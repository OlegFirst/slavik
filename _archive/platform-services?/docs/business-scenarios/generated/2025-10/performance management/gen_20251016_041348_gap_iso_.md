Title: Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Dashboard
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Dashboard provides real-time visibility into the effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This scenario enables organizations to continuously monitor, measure, analyze, and evaluate the performance of their BCM activities against established objectives and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The dashboard aggregates data from multiple BCM components including BIA completion rates, risk assessment updates, exercise participation, incident response times, and recovery objective achievements. By providing comprehensive performance metrics and trend analysis, organizations can identify areas requiring improvement, demonstrate compliance with ISO 22301 requirements, and make data-driven decisions to enhance their BCM program effectiveness.

**Inputs**:
- Performance metrics configuration (KPIs, thresholds, measurement periods)
- BIA completion and update statistics
- Risk assessment scores and mitigation progress
- Exercise and testing results data
- Incident response metrics and recovery times
- Training completion rates and competency scores
- Audit findings and corrective action status
- Recovery objective achievement rates (RTO/RPO)

**Outputs**:
- Real-time performance dashboard with visual indicators
- KPI achievement reports with trend analysis
- Performance evaluation summary reports
- Compliance status indicators by ISO clause
- Automated alerts for performance threshold breaches
- Executive performance scorecards
- Improvement opportunity recommendations
- Historical performance comparison charts

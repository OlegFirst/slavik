Title: Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive performance monitoring framework to track and measure the effectiveness of its Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This scenario establishes systematic monitoring of key performance indicators (KPIs), recovery time objectives (RTOs), and recovery point objectives (RPOs) across all critical business processes identified during the Business Impact Analysis.

The framework enables real-time tracking of BCMS performance metrics, including exercise completion rates, incident response times, training participation levels, and audit findings resolution. Automated dashboards provide visibility into performance trends, enabling management to identify areas requiring improvement and make data-driven decisions about resource allocation and process optimization.

**Inputs**:
- Business continuity KPI definitions
- Performance measurement criteria
- Data collection intervals
- Threshold values for alerts
- Historical performance data
- Exercise and test results
- Incident response metrics
- Training completion records

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement dashboard
- KPI trend analysis reports
- Performance deviation alerts
- Compliance scorecards
- Executive performance summaries
- Improvement opportunity reports

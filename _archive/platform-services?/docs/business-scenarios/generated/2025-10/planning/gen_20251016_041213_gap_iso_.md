Title: Operational Planning and Control Implementation
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish, implement, and maintain processes needed to meet business continuity requirements and implement actions identified during planning activities. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system enables organizations to plan and control their business continuity operations by defining operational criteria, implementing control measures, and maintaining documented information as evidence of process execution.

The system provides a structured approach to operational planning by integrating risk treatment actions, business continuity strategies, and resource requirements into actionable operational controls. This ensures that planned arrangements are implemented effectively and that any changes to operations are managed in a controlled manner to prevent unintended consequences to business continuity capabilities.

**Inputs**:
- Risk assessment results and treatment plans
- Business continuity strategies and solutions
- Resource requirements and constraints
- Performance objectives and criteria
- Regulatory and contractual requirements
- Lessons learned from exercises and incidents

**Outputs**:
- Operational control procedures
- Process performance metrics
- Resource allocation schedules
- Control implementation status reports
- Change control records
- Documented evidence of control effectiveness

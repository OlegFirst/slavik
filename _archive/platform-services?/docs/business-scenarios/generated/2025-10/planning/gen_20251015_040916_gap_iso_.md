Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Business Continuity Strategy Development process enables organizations to establish and maintain appropriate continuity strategies based on the results of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and Risk Assessment. This systematic approach ensures that recovery strategies are aligned with business priorities, recovery time objectives, and available resources.

The process involves evaluating multiple strategy options for critical business activities, considering factors such as cost-effectiveness, implementation complexity, and recovery capabilities. Strategy selection is based on predefined criteria including Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD), Recovery Time Objectives (RTO), and minimum resource requirements identified during the BIA process.

This scenario demonstrates compliance by ensuring that business continuity strategies are formally developed, documented, and approved through a structured workflow. The process maintains traceability between business requirements and selected strategies, enabling organizations to justify their continuity approach and demonstrate that appropriate measures are in place to protect prioritized activities.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results (critical activities, RTOs, RPOs, dependencies)
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Resource availability constraints
- Budget parameters
- Regulatory requirements
- Existing infrastructure capabilities
- Stakeholder preferences

**Outputs**:
- Approved BC strategies document
- Strategy selection justification matrix
- Resource allocation plan
- Implementation roadmap
- Cost-benefit analysis report
- Strategy effectiveness metrics

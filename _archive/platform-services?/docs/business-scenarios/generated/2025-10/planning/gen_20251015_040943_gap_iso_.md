Title: Business Continuity Objectives Setting and Review

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains business continuity objectives that are consistent with its business continuity policy and strategic direction. These objectives are set at relevant functions and levels within the organization, taking into account the results of business impact analysis and risk assessment. The system enables systematic definition, tracking, and review of measurable BC objectives that align with organizational priorities and compliance requirements.

This scenario demonstrates how the organization defines SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) business continuity objectives, assigns ownership, and monitors progress through automated dashboards and reporting. The process ensures that BC objectives are communicated throughout the organization and regularly reviewed for continued relevance and effectiveness.

**Inputs**:
- Strategic business priorities
- Business continuity policy requirements
- BIA results and critical activity priorities
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Resource availability constraints
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Previous objective performance data

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC objectives with success criteria
- Objective ownership assignments
- Performance measurement indicators
- Progress tracking dashboards
- Objective review schedule
- Achievement reports and gap analysis

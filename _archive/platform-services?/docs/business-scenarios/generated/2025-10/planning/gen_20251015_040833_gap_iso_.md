Title: Operational Planning and Control Implementation

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish, implement and maintain processes needed to meet business continuity requirements and implement actions identified during planning activities. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system enables organizations to operationalize their business continuity planning through structured implementation of controls, resource allocation, and performance criteria establishment.

The system provides comprehensive operational planning capabilities that ensure business continuity strategies are effectively translated into actionable plans with clear controls, responsibilities, and measurable outcomes. This includes managing the implementation of risk treatments, recovery strategies, and ensuring adequate resources are allocated and maintained for business continuity operations.

**Inputs**:
- Business Impact Analysis results
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Recovery strategies and objectives
- Resource requirements and constraints
- Performance criteria and targets
- Regulatory and contractual requirements
- Lessons learned from exercises and incidents

**Outputs**:
- Operational control procedures
- Resource allocation plans
- Implementation schedules and milestones
- Performance indicators and metrics
- Control effectiveness reports
- Resource readiness assessments
- Compliance status dashboards

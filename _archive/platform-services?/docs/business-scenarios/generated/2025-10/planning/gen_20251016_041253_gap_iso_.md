Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and Risk Assessment. Organizations must identify and evaluate multiple strategy options for protecting prioritized activities, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery objectives.

The process involves analyzing resource requirements, evaluating alternative approaches, and selecting optimal strategies that meet Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO). Strategy selection must consider dependencies, single points of failure, and the organization's risk appetite while ensuring strategies are practical and maintainable.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results with prioritized activities
- Risk assessment findings
- Resource requirements analysis
- Recovery objectives (RTO/RPO)
- Budget constraints
- Regulatory requirements
- Stakeholder requirements

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategies
- Strategy selection rationale
- Resource allocation plan
- Implementation roadmap
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Strategy approval records

Service: BIA

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic execution of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and document critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. The process involves analyzing business functions, determining their criticality based on impact over time, and establishing recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) for each critical activity.

The BIA execution process ensures comprehensive assessment of all business activities, their interdependencies, and resource requirements. This systematic approach enables the organization to prioritize recovery efforts and allocate resources effectively during disruptions. The analysis considers various impact categories including financial, operational, legal, regulatory, and reputational consequences of disruption.

**Inputs**:
- Business unit identification
- Activity inventory list
- Impact assessment criteria
- Time-based impact thresholds
- Resource dependency mapping
- Stakeholder interview data
- Historical incident data
- Regulatory requirements

**Outputs**:
- Critical activity register
- RTO/RPO assignments
- Resource requirement matrix
- Dependency mapping report
- Impact assessment summary
- Priority recovery sequence
- BIA executive report

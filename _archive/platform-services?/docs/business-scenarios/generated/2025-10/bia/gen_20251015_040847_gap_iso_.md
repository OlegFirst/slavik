**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic documentation and validation of business impact analysis requirements in accordance with ISO 22301 Clause 8.2. The organization conducts a comprehensive review of all critical business functions, their dependencies, and recovery requirements to establish documented criteria for business continuity planning.

The process involves gathering detailed requirements from business units, analyzing interdependencies between processes, and documenting recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs). This ensures that all business continuity requirements are properly identified, documented, and validated by relevant stakeholders before being incorporated into the business continuity management system.

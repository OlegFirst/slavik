Title: Business Impact Analysis Execution and Documentation
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic execution of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and document critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. The organization conducts comprehensive BIA assessments across all business units to determine the potential impacts of disruptions on operations, establishing recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) for each critical activity.

The BIA process involves structured interviews with process owners, analysis of interdependencies between business functions, and evaluation of resource requirements for continuity. This systematic approach ensures that the organization understands its critical activities and can prioritize recovery efforts based on documented impact assessments, time-critical requirements, and resource dependencies.

**Inputs**:
- Business unit identification
- Process inventory list
- Stakeholder interview schedules
- Historical incident data
- Resource dependency mappings
- Financial impact thresholds
- Regulatory compliance requirements

**Outputs**:

- RTO/RPO documentation
- Resource requirement analysis
- Dependency mapping diagrams
- Priority recovery sequence
- BIA summary report

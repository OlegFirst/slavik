Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to systematically identify, analyze, and address risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This framework enables proactive identification of internal and external issues that may affect the organization's ability to achieve its BCMS objectives and deliver products and services during disruptions.

The assessment process integrates with strategic planning cycles and considers both negative risks (threats) and positive risks (opportunities) across all business units and processes. Regular reviews ensure that emerging risks are captured and that the organization can adapt its BCMS to changing circumstances, maintaining resilience and continuous improvement.

**Inputs**:
- Internal and external context analysis
- Previous risk assessment results
- Business impact analysis data
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Historical incident data
- Industry threat intelligence
- BCMS performance metrics

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports
- Risk treatment plans
- Opportunity implementation roadmaps
- Risk acceptance documentation
- Updated BCMS objectives
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Management review presentations

Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to systematically identify, analyze, and address risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This framework enables proactive management of potential disruptions while identifying opportunities for BCMS improvement and enhanced organizational resilience.

The assessment process integrates with strategic planning cycles and operational reviews, ensuring that risks and opportunities are evaluated in the context of both internal and external factors. This includes consideration of interested party requirements, changing business environment, technological advances, and lessons learned from incidents. The framework supports evidence-based decision-making for resource allocation and BCMS enhancement initiatives.

**Inputs**:
- Internal and external context analysis
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Previous risk assessment results
- Incident and disruption history
- Compliance obligations
- Strategic objectives
- Resource constraints
- Technology landscape changes

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk treatment plans
- Opportunity implementation roadmap
- Risk assessment reports
- Priority matrix for actions
- Resource allocation recommendations
- BCMS improvement initiatives

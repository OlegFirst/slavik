Title: Management Review Performance Evaluation
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Management Review Performance Evaluation scenario enables organizations to conduct systematic reviews of their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) at planned intervals. This scenario facilitates top management's evaluation of the BCMS's continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness, including assessment of opportunities for improvement and the need for changes to the BCMS.

The scenario automates the collection and presentation of key performance data, audit results, and stakeholder feedback for management review meetings. It ensures that all required inputs for management review are systematically gathered, analyzed, and presented in a format that enables informed decision-making. The system tracks review outcomes, decisions, and action items to ensure continuous improvement of the BCMS.

**Inputs**:
- Review period (start and end dates)
- Previous management review outcomes and action items
- Internal audit results and findings
- External audit results (if applicable)
- BCMS performance metrics and KPIs
- Incident reports and lessons learned
- Changes in internal and external issues
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Resource adequacy assessment data
- Risk assessment updates
- Compliance status reports

**Outputs**:
- Management review agenda and supporting documentation
- Performance evaluation dashboard
- Trend analysis reports
- Decision log with improvement opportunities
- Action plan with assigned responsibilities and deadlines
- Updated BCMS objectives and targets
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Management review minutes and attendance records
- Communication plan for review outcomes

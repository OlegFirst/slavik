Title: Management Review Performance Evaluation
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Management Review Performance Evaluation scenario enables organizations to conduct comprehensive management reviews of their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) at planned intervals. This systematic evaluation process ensures that the BCMS remains suitable, adequate, and effective in achieving its intended outcomes while identifying opportunities for continual improvement.

The scenario facilitates the collection, analysis, and presentation of key performance data, audit results, and stakeholder feedback to top management. It provides a structured framework for evaluating the BCMS performance against established objectives, reviewing resource adequacy, and making informed decisions about necessary changes or improvements. The automated workflow ensures all required inputs are considered and documented, supporting evidence-based decision-making and demonstrating leadership commitment to business continuity.

**Inputs**:
- Previous management review actions and status
- Internal audit results and findings
- External audit results (if applicable)
- BCMS performance metrics and KPIs
- Incident and crisis response evaluations
- Exercise and test results
- Risk assessment updates
- Compliance status reports
- Resource utilization data
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Changes in external/internal context

**Outputs**:
- Management review meeting minutes
- Decision log with assigned actions
- Resource allocation recommendations
- BCMS improvement opportunities report
- Updated strategic direction document
- Performance evaluation dashboard
- Action plan with timelines and owners
- Communication plan for review outcomes

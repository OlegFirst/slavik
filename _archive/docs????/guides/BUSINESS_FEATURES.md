### 2. 📊 Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

**What it does**: Identifies critical business processes and their dependencies.

**6-Step Wizard**:
1. **Planning** - Define scope and objectives
2. **Process Selection** - Choose critical processes (AI suggests based on industry)
3. **Data Collection** - Gather impact data (AI-assisted questionnaires)
4. **Dependency Mapping** - Visual map of dependencies (AI auto-discovers)
5. **Impact Analysis** - Calculate financial impact, RTO, RPO
6. **Report Generation** - Professional BIA report (auto-generated)

**AI Features**:
- 🤖 **AI Recommendations** - "Based on your industry, these 12 processes are typically critical"
- 🔍 **Auto-Discovery** - AI finds hidden dependencies (e.g., "Your payment system depends on email server")
- 💰 **Impact Calculator** - AI estimates financial impact: "$50K/hour downtime for Process X"
- ⏰ **RTO/RPO Suggestions** - AI recommends optimal recovery objectives

**Business Value**:
- ⏱️ **Time Saved**: 70% faster than manual BIA (2 weeks vs 6 weeks)
- 💰 **Cost Savings**: Automated data collection reduces consultant costs
- 🎯 **Accuracy**: AI catches 30% more dependencies than manual analysis

**Real example**:
> "A hospital used BIA and discovered their patient records system depended on a 3rd-party email service that had no SLA. They fixed this before an incident occurred."

**What it does**: Tests BC plans without disrupting business.

**Exercise Types**:

- 🚶 **Walkthrough** - Step-through procedures
- 🎮 **Simulation** - Digital Twin (no real impact!)
- 🔥 **Full-scale** - Actual failover (scheduled)

**Digital Twin Simulation** (Unique Feature):
- 🖥️ **Virtual Environment** - Exact copy of your IT systems
- 🔥 **Inject Scenarios** - "What if datacenter fails?"
- ⏱️ **Measure RTO** - Did you recover in time?
- 📊 **AI Insights** - "Your recovery took 4 hours, RTO is 2 hours - here's why"

**After Action Report (AAR)**:
- 📋 Automated AAR generation
- 📊 Metrics: RTO achieved, gaps found, lessons learned
- ✅ **Action Items** - AI creates action items from gaps
- 📈 **Trend Analysis** - "Your RTO improved by 30% since last exercise"

**Business Value**:
- 💰 **No Downtime**: Test without disrupting business
- 🎯 **Find Gaps**: Discover issues before real incidents
- 📊 **Prove Compliance**: Evidence for auditors

**Real example**:
> "An e-commerce company ran Digital Twin exercise and discovered their backup datacenter was missing a critical firewall rule. Fixed before Black Friday. Saved potential $5M in lost sales."

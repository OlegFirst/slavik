# ISO 22301:2019 - Business Continuity Management Systems
## Clauses Breakdown (Detailed Requirements)

**Standard:** ISO 22301:2019
**Title:** Security and resilience — Business continuity management systems — Requirements
**Based on:** PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) continuous improvement cycle

---

## STRUCTURE

- **Clauses 0-3**: Introduction (informative, non-mandatory)
- **Clauses 4-10**: Requirements (mandatory for certification)

---

## CLAUSE 4: CONTEXT OF THE ORGANIZATION

### 4.1 Understanding the organization and its context

**Requirement:**
- Determine external and internal issues relevant to BCMS
- Consider legal, regulatory, financial, technological, competitive, market, social, cultural environment

**Evidence needed:**
- Context analysis document
- PESTLE or SWOT analysis
- Stakeholder analysis

**Audit questions:**
- How does the organization identify internal/external factors affecting BC?
- Are these factors documented and reviewed regularly?

### 4.2 Understanding the needs and expectations of interested parties

**Requirement:**
- Identify interested parties (stakeholders)
- Determine their requirements and expectations
- Understand which requirements become compliance obligations

**Interested parties examples:**
- Customers, regulators, suppliers, employees, shareholders, community

**Evidence needed:**
- Stakeholder register
- Requirements documentation
- Compliance obligations list

### 4.3 Determining the scope of the BCMS

**Requirement:**
- Define boundaries and applicability
- Consider context, interested parties, products/services
- Document scope clearly

**Evidence needed:**
- BCMS scope statement (documented)
- Justification for any exclusions

**Audit questions:**
- Is scope clearly defined and documented?
- Does scope cover all critical operations?

### 4.4 Business continuity management system

**Requirement:**
- Establish, implement, maintain, continually improve BCMS
- Include processes and interactions

## CLAUSE 5: LEADERSHIP

### 5.1 Leadership and commitment

**Requirement:**
- Top management demonstrates leadership and commitment
- Ensure BCMS policy and objectives are established
- Ensure resources available
- Communicate importance of effective BCMS

**Evidence needed:**
- Management meeting minutes
- Resource allocation records
- Communication records

**Audit questions:**
- How does top management demonstrate commitment to BCMS?
- Are adequate resources allocated?

### 5.2 Policy

**Requirement:**
- Establish BC policy that:
  - Appropriate to purpose and context
  - Provides framework for BC objectives
  - Includes commitment to satisfy requirements
  - Includes commitment to continual improvement

**Evidence needed:**
- BC Policy (documented, approved by top management)
- Policy communication records

### 5.3 Organizational roles, responsibilities and authorities

**Requirement:**
- Assign and communicate responsibilities and authorities for BCMS
- Define roles for:
  - Ensuring BCMS conforms to requirements
  - Reporting on BCMS performance to top management

**Evidence needed:**
- Organizational chart with BC roles
- Job descriptions with BC responsibilities
- RACI matrix

---

## CLAUSE 6: PLANNING

### 6.1 Actions to address risks and opportunities

**Requirement:**
- Determine risks and opportunities for BCMS
- Plan actions to address them
- Integrate actions into BCMS processes
- Evaluate effectiveness of actions

**Evidence needed:**
- Risk register
- Risk treatment plans
- Action plans

### 6.2 Business continuity objectives and planning to achieve them

**Requirement:**
- Establish BC objectives at relevant functions and levels
- Objectives must be:
  - Consistent with BC policy
  - Measurable
  - Monitored
  - Communicated
  - Updated as appropriate

**Evidence needed:**
- BC objectives documentation
- Measurement criteria
- Progress tracking

**Audit questions:**
- Are BC objectives defined and measurable?
- How are objectives monitored and reviewed?

### 6.3 Planning of changes

**Requirement:**
- When changes to BCMS are needed, carry them out in planned manner
- Consider purpose of changes and potential consequences

---

## CLAUSE 7: SUPPORT

### 7.1 Resources

**Requirement:**
- Determine and provide resources needed for BCMS:
  - People
  - Infrastructure
  - Technology
  - Financial resources

**Evidence needed:**
- Resource allocation documentation
- Budget for BC activities
- Infrastructure inventory

### 7.2 Competence

**Requirement:**
- Determine necessary competence for BC roles
- Ensure persons are competent (education, training, experience)
- Take actions to acquire competence
- Retain documented information as evidence

**Evidence needed:**
- Competency matrix
- Training records
- Qualifications/certifications
- Training plans

**Audit questions:**
- How is BC competence determined and maintained?
- Are training records maintained?

### 7.3 Awareness

**Requirement:**
- Ensure persons are aware of:
  - BC policy
  - Their contribution to BCMS effectiveness
  - Implications of not conforming

**Evidence needed:**
- Awareness campaign materials
- Communication records
- Awareness surveys

### 7.4 Communication

**Requirement:**
- Determine internal and external communications relevant to BCMS:
  - What to communicate
  - When to communicate
  - With whom to communicate
  - How to communicate
  - Who communicates

**Evidence needed:**
- Communication plan
- Communication logs
- Crisis communication procedures

### 7.5 Documented information

**Requirement:**
- BCMS must include:
  - Documented information required by standard
  - Documented information determined necessary for effectiveness
- Control documented information (creation, update, access, retention)

**Evidence needed:**
- Document control procedure
- Document register
- Version control
- Access controls

## CLAUSE 8: OPERATION

**This is the CORE of BCMS - implementing the plans**

### 8.1 Operational planning and control

**Requirement:**
- Plan, implement, control processes needed to meet requirements
- Implement actions from 6.1
- Keep documented information to demonstrate processes carried out

### 8.2 Business impact analysis and risk assessment

#### 8.2.1 General

**Requirement:**
- Establish, implement, maintain process for BIA and risk assessment

#### 8.2.2 Business impact analysis (BIA)

**Requirement:**
- Analyze impact of disruptions over time
- Identify activities supporting products/services
- Assess impacts over time if not resumed
- Establish time frames for resumption (RTO)
- Identify resources to support prioritized activities
- Identify dependencies and supporting activities

**Evidence needed:**
- BIA methodology document
- BIA reports for critical processes
- RTO/RPO definitions
- MTPD (Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption)
- Impact assessment (financial, operational, reputational, regulatory)
- Dependencies mapping

**Healthcare specific:**
- Patient impact analysis
- Clinical service prioritization
- Essential services identification (WHO framework)
- Regulatory compliance (HIPAA, local regulations)

**Audit questions:**
- How often is BIA conducted?
- Are all critical processes covered?
- Are RTOs realistic and tested?

#### 8.2.3 Risk assessment

**Requirement:**
- Identify risks of disruption
- Analyze and evaluate risks
- Identify risk treatment options

**Evidence needed:**
- Risk assessment methodology
- Risk register
- Risk analysis (likelihood × impact)
- Risk treatment plans

**Methodologies:**
- Qualitative (High/Medium/Low)
- Quantitative (FAIR, Monte Carlo)
- Scenario-based

### 8.3 Business continuity strategy

**Requirement:**
- Determine appropriate strategies based on BIA and risk assessment
- Select strategies for:
  - Pre-incident activities (prevention, mitigation)
  - During-incident activities (response)
  - Post-incident activities (recovery, resumption)

**Evidence needed:**
- BC strategy document
- Strategy options analysis
- Chosen strategies with justification
- Recovery strategies (work area recovery, technology recovery, people)

**Audit questions:**
- Are strategies aligned with RTOs?
- Are multiple scenarios considered?
- Are strategies cost-effective?

### 8.4 Business continuity plans and procedures

#### 8.4.1 General

**Requirement:**
- Establish, implement, maintain BC plans and procedures
- Ensure documented, available, understood

#### 8.4.2 Incident response structure

**Requirement:**
- Establish structure to respond to disruptive incidents
- Define:
  - Roles and responsibilities
  - Authority to act
  - Incident response process

**Evidence needed:**
- Incident response plan
- Call tree/escalation matrix
- Incident management team structure
- 24/7 contact information

#### 8.4.3 Warning and communication

**Requirement:**
- Establish, implement procedures to:
  - Detect incidents
  - Monitor and report
  - Warn and communicate with stakeholders

**Evidence needed:**
- Alert/notification procedures
- Communication templates
- Stakeholder contact lists
- Communication protocols (internal/external)

#### 8.4.4 Business continuity plans and procedures

**Requirement:**
- Develop BC plans covering:
  - Immediate actions during disruption
  - Continuity of prioritized activities
  - Resources needed
  - Dependencies
  - Communication
  - Escalation

**Evidence needed:**
- BC plans for each critical process
- Procedure documents (step-by-step)
- Resource lists (people, facilities, technology)
- Alternative site procedures
- Supplier continuity arrangements

### 8.5 Exercising and testing

**Requirement:**
- Exercise and test BC plans at planned intervals
- Base on:
  - Test objectives
  - Size and nature of organization
- Evaluate results and implement corrective actions

**Exercise types:**
- Desktop exercises (tabletop)
- Walkthroughs
- Simulations
- Full-scale exercises
- Component testing (technology recovery)

**Evidence needed:**
- Exercise schedule
- Exercise plans/scenarios
- Exercise reports
- Lessons learned
- Corrective action plans

**Audit questions:**
- How often are exercises conducted?
- Do exercises test all critical processes?
- Are lessons learned implemented?

## CLAUSE 9: PERFORMANCE EVALUATION

### 9.1 Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation

**Requirement:**
- Determine what to monitor and measure
- Determine methods for valid results
- Determine when to monitor and measure
- Determine who analyzes and evaluates
- Retain documented information

**Metrics examples:**
- % of critical processes with BC plans
- RTO achievement rate
- Exercise participation rate
- Training completion rate
- Incident response times
- Plan update frequency

**Evidence needed:**
- Performance metrics
- Measurement procedures
- Analysis reports
- Trend analysis

### 9.2 Internal audit

**Requirement:**
- Conduct internal audits at planned intervals
- Ensure BCMS:
  - Conforms to requirements
  - Is effectively implemented and maintained

**Audit process:**
1. Plan audit (scope, criteria, frequency, methods)
2. Select competent auditors
3. Ensure objectivity and impartiality
4. Report results to relevant management
5. Retain documented information

**Evidence needed:**
- Audit program/schedule
- Audit plans
- Audit checklists (clause-by-clause)
- Audit reports
- Nonconformity reports
- Corrective action plans

**Audit frequency:**
- Typically annual minimum
- More frequent for high-risk areas
- After major changes
- After incidents

### 9.3 Management review

**Requirement:**
- Top management reviews BCMS at planned intervals
- Ensure continuing suitability, adequacy, effectiveness

**Review inputs:**
- Status of actions from previous reviews
- Changes in external/internal issues
- Performance information (metrics, audit results, exercise results)
- Feedback from interested parties
- Opportunities for continual improvement

**Review outputs:**
- Decisions on improvement opportunities
- Decisions on changes to BCMS
- Resource needs

**Evidence needed:**
- Management review agenda
- Management review minutes
- Action items
- Follow-up documentation

## CLAUSE 10: IMPROVEMENT

### 10.1 Nonconformity and corrective action

**Requirement:**
- When nonconformity occurs:
  - React to it and take action
  - Evaluate need for action to eliminate causes
  - Implement corrective action
  - Review effectiveness
  - Update BCMS if necessary

**Evidence needed:**
- Nonconformity register
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective action plans
- Effectiveness reviews
- Closure evidence

**Audit questions:**
- How are nonconformities identified and recorded?
- Is root cause analysis performed?
- Are corrective actions effective?

### 10.2 Continual improvement

**Requirement:**
- Continually improve suitability, adequacy, effectiveness of BCMS
- Consider results of analysis and evaluation, management review

**Improvement examples:**
- Enhanced BC strategies
- Improved training programs
- Better technology solutions
- Streamlined processes
- Reduced RTOs
- Increased resilience

## PDCA MAPPING

**PLAN (Clauses 4-7):**
- Understand context, stakeholders, requirements
- Establish policy, objectives, resources

**DO (Clause 8):**
- Implement BIA, risk assessment
- Develop strategies and plans
- Exercise and test

**CHECK (Clause 9):**
- Monitor and measure
- Internal audit
- Management review

**ACT (Clause 10):**
- Corrective actions
- Continual improvement

---

## CERTIFICATION PROCESS

**Stage 1 Audit:** Documentation review
**Stage 2 Audit:** Implementation assessment
**Certification:** Valid for 3 years
**Surveillance Audits:** Annual (maintain certification)
**Recertification:** Every 3 years

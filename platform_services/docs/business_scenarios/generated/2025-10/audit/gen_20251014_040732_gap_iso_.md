Title: Internal Audit Program Management
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program to evaluate the effectiveness of their business continuity management system (BCMS). This scenario enables systematic planning, execution, and tracking of internal audits according to ISO 22301 requirements, ensuring all BCMS elements are regularly assessed for conformity and effectiveness.

The system provides automated audit scheduling based on risk levels, previous audit findings, and regulatory requirements. It facilitates audit team assignment, checklist management, evidence collection, and non-conformity tracking. Real-time dashboards provide visibility into audit coverage, findings trends, and corrective action status, enabling management to make informed decisions about BCMS improvements.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule parameters
- Risk assessment results
- Previous audit findings
- Audit scope and criteria
- Auditor qualifications and availability
- BCMS process documentation
- Performance metrics and KPIs

**Outputs**:
- Annual audit calendar
- Audit assignment notifications
- Customized audit checklists
- Audit reports with findings
- Non-conformity records
- Corrective action requests
- Audit performance metrics
- Management review reports

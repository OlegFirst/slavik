**Description**:
The Internal Audit Program Management scenario enables organizations to establish, implement, and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their business continuity management system (BCMS). This scenario provides a structured approach to planning, conducting, and reporting internal audits at planned intervals to determine whether the BCMS conforms to the organization's own requirements and ISO 22301 standards.

The system facilitates audit scheduling based on risk assessment results, previous audit findings, and organizational changes. It supports the entire audit lifecycle from annual program planning through individual audit execution, findings management, and corrective action tracking. The scenario ensures audit independence by managing auditor assignments, competency requirements, and conflict of interest declarations.

This comprehensive audit management approach provides objective evidence of BCMS effectiveness, identifies improvement opportunities, and ensures systematic verification of compliance with both internal policies and ISO 22301 requirements. The automated workflow ensures consistent audit methodology while maintaining flexibility for different audit scopes and objectives.

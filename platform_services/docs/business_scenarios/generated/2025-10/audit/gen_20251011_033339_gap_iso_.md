Title: Internal Audit Program Implementation and Execution
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains a comprehensive internal audit program to evaluate the effectiveness of its Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This scenario demonstrates the systematic approach to planning, conducting, and reporting internal audits at planned intervals to verify that the BCMS conforms to ISO 22301 requirements and the organization's own requirements.

The internal audit process encompasses all BCMS processes, from policy and objectives through to performance evaluation and improvement. Audits are conducted by competent personnel who are independent of the areas being audited, ensuring objectivity and impartiality. The program includes risk-based audit planning, standardized audit procedures, and formal reporting mechanisms to communicate findings to relevant management levels.

This systematic approach ensures continuous monitoring of BCMS effectiveness, identifies opportunities for improvement, and provides management with confidence that business continuity arrangements are properly implemented and maintained. The audit program serves as a critical feedback mechanism for the management review process and drives continual improvement of the BCMS.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule and plan
- Previous audit reports and corrective actions
- BCMS documentation (policies, procedures, plans)
- ISO 22301 requirements checklist
- Audit criteria and scope definition
- Resource allocation for audit activities

**Outputs**:
- Audit reports with findings and recommendations
- Non-conformity reports
- Corrective action requests
- Audit program performance metrics
- Updated risk register based on audit findings
- Audit trail and evidence documentation
- Management briefing presentations

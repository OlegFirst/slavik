Title: Business Continuity Objectives Setting and Review

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains business continuity objectives that are consistent with the business continuity policy and aligned with the organization's strategic direction. These objectives are set at relevant functions and levels within the organization, taking into account the results of the business impact analysis and risk assessment.

The system enables systematic definition, documentation, and tracking of measurable business continuity objectives. Each objective includes specific targets for recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objective (MBCO) for critical activities. The objectives are reviewed periodically and updated based on changes in business requirements, risk landscape, or organizational context.

**Inputs**:
- Business continuity policy requirements
- BIA results (critical activities, dependencies, RTOs, RPOs)
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Strategic business priorities
- Resource availability constraints
- Regulatory and contractual requirements

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC objectives matrix
- Objective achievement metrics
- Performance indicators dashboard
- Objective review schedule
- Gap analysis report
- Resource allocation recommendations

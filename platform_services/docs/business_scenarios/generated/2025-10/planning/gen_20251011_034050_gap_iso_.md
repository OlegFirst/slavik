Title: Management Review Performance Evaluation
Service: Planning
Category: Reports

**Description**:
The organization conducts systematic management reviews of the business continuity management system (BCMS) to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness. This scenario demonstrates the comprehensive evaluation of BCMS performance through structured management review meetings that assess key performance indicators, audit results, and improvement opportunities.

The management review process integrates data from multiple sources including internal audits, exercise results, incident reports, and performance metrics. Senior management evaluates this information to make informed decisions about resource allocation, strategic direction, and continuous improvement initiatives for the BCMS. The review ensures alignment with organizational objectives and identifies areas requiring management attention or system enhancement.

**Inputs**:
- Previous management review action items
- Internal audit reports and findings
- Exercise and test results
- Performance indicator data
- Incident and activation reports
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Risk assessment updates
- Compliance status reports
- Resource utilization metrics
- Change management records

**Outputs**:
- Management review meeting minutes
- Decision log with assigned actions
- Resource allocation recommendations
- BCMS improvement plan
- Updated strategic objectives
- Performance trend analysis
- Compliance dashboard
- Executive summary report

Title: Operational Planning and Control Implementation
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish, implement, and maintain processes needed to meet business continuity requirements and implement actions identified during planning activities. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system enables organizations to operationalize their business continuity planning through structured implementation of controls, resource allocation, and performance criteria.

The system provides a comprehensive framework for translating strategic business continuity objectives into operational reality. It ensures that planned arrangements are effectively implemented, controlled, and monitored through defined processes, clear responsibilities, and measurable performance indicators. This includes managing outsourced processes, controlling planned changes, and reviewing unintended changes to ensure business continuity capabilities remain effective.

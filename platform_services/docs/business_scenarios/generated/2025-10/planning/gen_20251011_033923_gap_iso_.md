Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization needs to develop and select appropriate business continuity strategies based on the results of business impact analysis and risk assessment. This scenario demonstrates the systematic approach to identifying, evaluating, and selecting strategies that protect prioritized activities and manage risks to an acceptable level.

The Planning Service facilitates the development of multiple strategy options for each critical business function, considering factors such as recovery time objectives, resource requirements, and cost-effectiveness. The system ensures that selected strategies are documented, approved by appropriate stakeholders, and aligned with the organization's risk appetite and business objectives.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results (critical activities, RTOs, RPOs)
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Resource availability constraints
- Budget parameters
- Regulatory requirements
- Stakeholder preferences
- Current capability assessment

**Outputs**:
- Strategy options matrix
- Cost-benefit analysis for each option
- Selected strategies documentation
- Resource requirements summary
- Implementation roadmap
- Approval records
- Strategy effectiveness metrics

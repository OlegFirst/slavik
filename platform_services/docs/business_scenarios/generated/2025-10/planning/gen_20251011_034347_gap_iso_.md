Title: Operational Planning and Control Implementation
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish, implement, and maintain processes needed to meet business continuity requirements and implement actions identified during planning activities. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system enables systematic operational planning and control by providing structured workflows for implementing BC strategies, managing resources, and controlling planned changes.

The system facilitates the translation of strategic business continuity objectives into operational plans with clear controls, resource allocations, and performance criteria. It ensures that all operational planning activities are documented, controlled, and aligned with the organization's risk appetite and recovery objectives. This includes managing outsourced processes that affect business continuity and ensuring adequate control measures are in place.

**Inputs**:
- Business continuity strategies and objectives
- Resource requirements and constraints
- Risk assessment results
- Recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO)
- Supplier and outsourcing dependencies
- Regulatory and contractual requirements
- Change request details

**Outputs**:
- Operational BC plans with defined controls
- Resource allocation matrices
- Process control procedures
- Outsourced process control documentation
- Change control records
- Performance criteria and metrics
- Operational readiness reports

Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
A multinational manufacturing company needs to establish comprehensive business continuity strategies for its critical operations identified through BIA. The organization must develop and select appropriate strategies that ensure continuity of prioritized activities within agreed recovery time objectives while considering resource requirements, implementation costs, and organizational capabilities.

The scenario involves analyzing different strategic options for maintaining operations during disruptions, including alternate facilities, remote working arrangements, outsourcing agreements, and technology redundancies. Each strategy must be evaluated against predefined criteria including feasibility, cost-effectiveness, recovery time capability, and alignment with business objectives to ensure optimal selection and implementation.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results with critical processes and RTOs
- Resource requirements and dependencies
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Budget constraints and financial parameters
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Current capability assessment
- Regulatory and contractual obligations

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategies for each critical process
- Strategy selection criteria and evaluation matrix
- Cost-benefit analysis for each strategy option
- Implementation roadmap and timelines
- Resource allocation plan
- Strategy approval documentation
- Performance indicators for strategy effectiveness

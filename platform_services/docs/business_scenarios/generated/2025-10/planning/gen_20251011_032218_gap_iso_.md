Title: Establish Operational Planning and Control Framework for BCM Activities

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
A multinational manufacturing company needs to establish comprehensive operational planning and control mechanisms to ensure their business continuity management system operates effectively across all facilities. The organization must define, implement, and maintain planned arrangements to meet BCM requirements while controlling both planned and unplanned changes that could impact continuity capabilities.

The scenario involves creating a structured framework that governs how BCM operational activities are planned, executed, monitored, and controlled. This includes establishing criteria for BCM processes, implementing controls in accordance with these criteria, maintaining documented information to demonstrate process execution, and managing outsourced processes that could affect business continuity. The framework ensures that all operational aspects of the BCMS are systematically managed and aligned with the organization's continuity objectives.

Title: Management Review Meeting Scheduling and Execution
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The BCM Governance module automates the scheduling, preparation, and execution of management review meetings as required by ISO 22301. The system tracks review cycles, automatically compiles performance data, and generates pre-meeting reports covering all aspects of the BCMS performance including audit results, incident statistics, and improvement opportunities.

The platform ensures that management reviews are conducted at planned intervals with comprehensive agenda items covering BCMS suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness. It facilitates the documentation of review inputs, discussions, decisions, and resulting actions, maintaining a complete audit trail for compliance demonstration.

**Inputs**:
- Review frequency settings (quarterly/semi-annual/annual)
- Previous review action items and status
- BCMS performance metrics and KPIs
- Internal audit findings and recommendations
- External audit results and observations
- Incident and crisis response statistics
- Resource utilization reports
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Changes in internal/external context
- Risk assessment updates
- Improvement suggestions from staff

**Outputs**:
- Management review meeting invitations
- Pre-meeting briefing package
- Performance dashboard with trend analysis
- Meeting minutes and attendance records
- Decision log with assigned responsibilities
- Action plan with deadlines and owners
- Post-review communication materials
- Updated BCMS objectives and targets
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Continuous improvement register updates

**Events Triggered**:
- review.meeting.scheduled
- review.package.generated
- review.meeting.completed
- review.actions.assigned
- review.decisions.documented

**Components**:
Service → Review Scheduler → Data Aggregator → Report Generator → Meeting Manager → Action Tracker → Communication Engine

**Compliance Value**: Ensures systematic management review of the BCMS at planned intervals, evaluating its continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness while identifying opportunities for improvement and need for changes.

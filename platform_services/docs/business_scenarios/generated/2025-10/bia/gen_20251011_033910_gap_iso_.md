**Description**:
A multinational manufacturing company needs to conduct a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis to identify and document critical resource requirements for maintaining business continuity. The organization operates across multiple regions with complex supply chains, IT infrastructure, and human resource dependencies that must be analyzed to determine minimum resource levels needed during disruptions.

The BIA process systematically evaluates each business function to identify essential resources including personnel, technology, facilities, suppliers, and information required to maintain operations at acceptable levels. This analysis establishes recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs) while documenting interdependencies between business units and external stakeholders.

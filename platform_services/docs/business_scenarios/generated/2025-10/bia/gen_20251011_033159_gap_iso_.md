Title: Conduct Business Impact Analysis for Critical Business Activities

Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
A financial services organization needs to identify and analyze its critical business activities to understand the impact of disruptions on operations, regulatory compliance, and customer service delivery. The organization operates multiple business units including retail banking, corporate lending, and investment services, each with varying levels of criticality and interdependencies.

The business impact analysis process will systematically evaluate each business activity to determine maximum tolerable period of disruption (MTPD), recovery time objectives (RTO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). This analysis will provide the foundation for developing appropriate business continuity strategies and allocating resources based on criticality levels.

**Inputs**:
- Business activity inventory
- Process dependency mappings
- Historical incident data
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Customer service level agreements
- Financial impact thresholds
- Operational resource requirements

**Outputs**:
- Critical activity identification report
- Impact assessment matrix
- MTPD/RTO/RPO definitions
- Resource requirement analysis
- Dependency mapping documentation
- Priority ranking of business activities
- Risk exposure assessment

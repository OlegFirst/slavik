Title: Implement Business Impact Analysis and Risk Assessment Process
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
A multinational manufacturing company needs to establish a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and risk assessment process to identify critical business activities, their dependencies, and potential disruptions. The organization operates across multiple regions with complex supply chains and must ensure business continuity for its key products and services.

The scenario involves conducting systematic analysis of all business functions to determine their criticality, recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). This process will identify resource requirements, interdependencies, and prioritize activities based on their impact on the organization's objectives and stakeholder requirements.

**Inputs**:
- Business function inventory
- Process maps and workflows
- Historical incident data
- Resource dependency matrices
- Stakeholder requirements
- Regulatory compliance obligations
- Financial impact thresholds
- Operational capacity data

**Outputs**:
- Critical activity register
- RTO/RPO/MBCO definitions
- Resource requirement analysis
- Dependency mapping report
- Risk assessment matrix
- Priority recovery sequence
- Impact analysis summary
- Risk treatment plans

Title: Implement Business Impact Analysis for Critical Business Functions

Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
A multinational financial services company needs to establish a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) process to identify and prioritize its critical business functions. The organization must determine the maximum acceptable outage times for each function and understand the potential impacts of disruptions on operations, reputation, and regulatory compliance.

The BIA process will systematically evaluate all business functions across departments, assess their criticality based on financial, operational, and reputational impacts, and establish recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs). This analysis will form the foundation for developing appropriate business continuity strategies and allocating resources effectively to protect the most critical operations.

**Inputs**:
- Business function inventory list
- Financial impact thresholds
- Operational dependency mappings
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Historical incident data
- Stakeholder criticality assessments
- Resource requirement specifications

**Outputs**:
- Critical business function register
- Maximum Acceptable Outage (MAO) times
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- Impact assessment reports
- Resource requirement analysis
- Dependency mapping documentation

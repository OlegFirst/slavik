**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic documentation and validation of business impact analysis requirements in accordance with ISO 22301 Clause 8.2. The organization conducts a comprehensive review of all critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements to establish documented criteria for business continuity planning.

The process involves gathering detailed requirements from business units, documenting recovery time objectives (RTOs), recovery point objectives (RPOs), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCOs). All requirements are validated through stakeholder consultation and formally documented to ensure they align with organizational objectives and regulatory obligations. This creates a traceable foundation for all subsequent business continuity planning activities.

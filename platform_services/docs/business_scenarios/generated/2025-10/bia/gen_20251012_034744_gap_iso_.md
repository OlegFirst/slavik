**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic approach to documenting and validating business impact analysis requirements in accordance with ISO 22301 Clause 8.2. The process ensures that all critical business functions, their dependencies, and recovery requirements are properly identified, documented, and validated through stakeholder engagement and evidence-based analysis.

The scenario encompasses the complete lifecycle of BIA requirements gathering, from initial stakeholder interviews through to the production of validated BIA reports. This includes identifying critical activities, determining maximum tolerable periods of disruption (MTPD), recovery time objectives (RTO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). The process ensures that all requirements are traceable, measurable, and aligned with organizational objectives.

Service: BIA

**Description**:
Organizations must establish and maintain documented requirements for conducting business impact analysis to identify critical activities and their dependencies. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system captures, validates, and manages BIA requirements including recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO) for all critical business functions.

The system enables business units to define their operational requirements through structured questionnaires and workshops, automatically validating inputs against organizational policies and industry benchmarks. Requirements are continuously reviewed and updated based on changing business needs, ensuring that the BIA remains aligned with organizational objectives and stakeholder expectations.

**Inputs**:
- Business function inventory
- Criticality assessment criteria
- Maximum tolerable period of disruption (MTPD)
- Resource dependency mappings
- Stakeholder impact thresholds
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Historical incident data

**Outputs**:
- Validated RTO/RPO requirements matrix
- Critical activity register
- Resource requirement specifications
- Dependency mapping documentation
- Compliance requirement checklist
- BIA requirements approval workflow

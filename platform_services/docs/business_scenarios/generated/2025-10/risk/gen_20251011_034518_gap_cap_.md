**Description**:
Business continuity managers need to systematically identify, assess, and prioritize risks across their organization. This capability enables users to create comprehensive risk assessments using standardized methodologies, automatically calculate risk scores based on likelihood and impact criteria, and generate risk heat maps for executive reporting. The system streamlines the risk assessment process while ensuring consistency and compliance with industry standards.

**Inputs**:
- Risk identification data (threat sources, vulnerabilities, assets at risk)
- Likelihood ratings (1-5 scale or custom probability percentages)
- Impact assessments across multiple categories (financial, operational, reputational, regulatory)
- Risk tolerance thresholds and appetite statements
- Control effectiveness ratings
- Risk owner assignments

**Outputs**:
- Automated risk scores (inherent and residual)
- Risk heat map visualization
- Risk register with prioritized risks
- Risk treatment recommendations
- Compliance gap analysis reports
- Risk trend analytics over time
- Executive dashboard with key risk indicators (KRIs)

**Events**:
- Risk score exceeds tolerance threshold → Alert to risk owner and escalation to management
- New risk identified → Notification to relevant stakeholders for assessment
- Risk assessment due for review → Automated reminder to risk owners
- Control effectiveness changes → Recalculation of residual risk scores
- Risk status changes → Update to risk register and audit trail

**Components**:
1. Risk identification module captures and categorizes risks
2. Assessment engine calculates scores using configured methodology
3. Risk register database stores all risk data with version control
4. Visualization engine generates heat maps and dashboards
5. Workflow engine manages review cycles and approvals
6. Integration APIs connect with GRC and incident management systems
7. Reporting module produces compliance and executive reports

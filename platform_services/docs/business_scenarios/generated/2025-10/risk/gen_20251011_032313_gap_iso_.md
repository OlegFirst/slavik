Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment for BCMS Planning
Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
An organization implements a systematic approach to identify and assess risks and opportunities that could impact the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) objectives. The scenario involves conducting quarterly risk assessments across all business units, evaluating both internal and external factors that could affect business continuity capabilities.

The assessment process includes analyzing potential disruptions, regulatory changes, technological vulnerabilities, and opportunities for improvement. Risk owners collaborate with the BCM team to evaluate likelihood and impact ratings, develop treatment plans, and monitor emerging threats. This proactive approach ensures the organization can address risks before they materialize and capitalize on opportunities to enhance resilience.

**Inputs**:
- Business context and environment analysis
- Previous risk assessment results
- Incident reports and lessons learned
- Regulatory and compliance requirements
- Stakeholder concerns and expectations
- Business impact analysis data
- External threat intelligence

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment matrix with ratings
- Risk treatment plans and action items
- Opportunity implementation roadmap
- Executive risk dashboard
- Quarterly risk assessment report

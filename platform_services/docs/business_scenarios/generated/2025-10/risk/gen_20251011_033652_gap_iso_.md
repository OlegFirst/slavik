Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework
Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario implements a comprehensive framework for identifying, assessing, and addressing risks and opportunities that could impact the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). The system enables organizations to proactively determine internal and external issues that may affect their ability to achieve intended BCMS outcomes, including both negative impacts (risks) and positive impacts (opportunities).

The framework integrates with organizational context analysis, stakeholder requirements, and strategic objectives to ensure that all potential risks and opportunities are systematically evaluated. It provides automated workflows for risk assessment, treatment planning, and monitoring of actions taken to address identified risks and opportunities, ensuring continuous improvement of the BCMS effectiveness.

**Inputs**:
- Organizational context data (internal/external factors)
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Previous risk assessment results
- Business impact analysis data
- Compliance obligations
- Strategic objectives
- Historical incident data
- Industry threat intelligence

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports with ratings
- Treatment plans with assigned actions
- Risk heat maps and dashboards
- Opportunity realization plans
- Residual risk analysis
- Management review reports
- Action tracking status

Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to systematically identify, analyze, and address risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This framework enables proactive management of uncertainties that may affect the organization's ability to achieve its business continuity objectives and maintain critical operations during disruptions.

The system captures both internal and external factors, including changes in business environment, regulatory requirements, technology landscape, and stakeholder expectations. Through structured assessment processes, the organization evaluates potential impacts on business continuity capabilities and determines appropriate actions to enhance resilience while capitalizing on improvement opportunities.

This systematic approach ensures that risks are not only identified and mitigated but also that opportunities for improving the BCMS effectiveness are recognized and pursued, supporting continuous improvement and strategic alignment with organizational objectives.

**Inputs**:
- Risk assessment criteria and methodology
- Business impact analysis results
- Internal and external context factors
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Previous incident reports and lessons learned
- Compliance obligations and regulatory changes
- Resource availability and constraints
- Technology and infrastructure dependencies

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports with impact and likelihood ratings
- Treatment plans for identified risks
- Opportunity realization strategies
- Risk acceptance documentation
- Updated business continuity strategies
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Performance improvement initiatives

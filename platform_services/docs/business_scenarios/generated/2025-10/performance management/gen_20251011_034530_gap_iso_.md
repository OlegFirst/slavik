Title: Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework establishes systematic processes for monitoring, measuring, analyzing, and evaluating the business continuity management system's performance. This scenario implements comprehensive performance indicators, measurement criteria, and evaluation mechanisms to ensure the BCMS meets its intended outcomes and continuously improves its effectiveness.

The framework integrates automated data collection from various BCM activities, real-time performance dashboards, and analytical tools to provide insights into system performance. It enables organizations to track compliance levels, identify performance gaps, measure recovery capabilities, and assess the overall maturity of their business continuity program against defined objectives and regulatory requirements.

**Inputs**:
- Performance indicators definition
- Measurement criteria and thresholds
- Data collection schedules
- Evaluation parameters
- Baseline performance metrics
- Monitoring frequency settings
- Stakeholder reporting requirements

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement reports
- KPI dashboards
- Trend analysis charts
- Compliance scorecards
- Performance gap analysis
- Improvement recommendations
- Executive performance summaries

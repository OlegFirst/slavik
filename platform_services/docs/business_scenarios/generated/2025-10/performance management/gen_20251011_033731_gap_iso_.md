Title: Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework establishes systematic processes for monitoring, measuring, analyzing, and evaluating the business continuity management system's performance. This scenario implements comprehensive performance tracking mechanisms that capture both quantitative metrics and qualitative assessments across all BCM activities, ensuring continuous improvement and effectiveness of the BCMS.

The framework integrates real-time monitoring dashboards, automated KPI calculations, and trend analysis capabilities to provide management with actionable insights into BCMS performance. By establishing clear performance criteria, measurement intervals, and evaluation methods, organizations can demonstrate compliance with ISO 22301's requirements for performance evaluation while identifying opportunities for improvement and ensuring the BCMS remains fit for purpose.

**Inputs**:
- BCM objectives and targets
- Performance indicators definition
- Measurement criteria and methods
- Data collection intervals
- Baseline performance values
- Evaluation thresholds
- Reporting requirements

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement dashboard
- KPI trend analysis reports
- Performance evaluation results
- Improvement recommendations
- Compliance scorecards
- Management review inputs
- Performance deviation alerts

Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework
Service: Risk Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive framework for identifying and addressing risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This systematic approach evaluates both internal and external factors that may affect the organization's ability to achieve its BCM objectives, including threats to operations, opportunities for improvement, and changes in the business environment.

The framework integrates risk assessment with strategic planning, ensuring that identified risks are properly evaluated for their potential impact on business continuity. It also identifies opportunities to enhance resilience, improve response capabilities, and strengthen the overall BCMS. This proactive approach enables the organization to anticipate and prepare for potential disruptions while capitalizing on opportunities to improve BCM effectiveness.

**Inputs**:
- Organizational context and strategic objectives
- Previous risk assessment results
- Internal and external audit findings
- Incident and disruption history
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Industry trends and threat intelligence

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk treatment plans
- Opportunity implementation roadmap
- Risk assessment reports with impact analysis
- Prioritized action items
- Resource allocation recommendations
- BCMS improvement initiatives

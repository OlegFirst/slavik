## PRESENTATION DELIVERY TIPS

### Timing (60-minute version)
- Slides 1-3: Context & Challenge (10 min)
- Slides 4-5: Problem Definition (5 min)
- Slides 6-12: Solution & Platform (20 min)
- Slides 13-15: Implementation & Evidence (10 min)
- Slides 16-20: Call to Action & Close (10 min)
- Q&A: (15 min)

### Key Messaging
1. **Lead with healthcare mission**: This is about serving communities, not technology
2. **Ground in evidence**: PHC Ukraine case shows real costs of traditional approach
3. **Emphasize equity**: Democratizing expertise is the core value proposition
4. **Be realistic**: Acknowledge challenges, but show clear path forward
5. **Inspire action**: Every audience member should leave knowing what they can do

### Visual Design Principles
- **Clean and professional**: Healthcare audience expects credibility
- **Data visualization**: Use charts/graphs for cost comparisons, timelines
- **Minimal text**: Speaker notes contain details, slides show key points only
- **Consistent branding**: NASH 4.0 + ISO 22301 + IAS 2025
- **Accessible**: High contrast, readable fonts, colorblind-friendly palette

### Questions to Anticipate
1. "How do you ensure AI recommendations are safe?"
2. "What about data privacy and sovereignty?"
3. "Can this work in low-resource settings?"
4. "How do you measure success?"
5. "What if my organization can't afford even €12K/year?"
6. "How is this different from other BCM tools?"
7. "What's your business model sustainability?"

See Q&A Scenarios document for detailed responses.

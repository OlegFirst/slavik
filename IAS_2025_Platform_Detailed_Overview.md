## THE CRISIS WE WITNESSED

### Ukraine's Public Health Center Under Fire

In 2022-2024, we provided technical support to Ukraine's Center for Public Health (PHC), which operates the national laboratory network for HIV, tuberculosis, and COVID-19 diagnostics. The organization faced:

- **Missile attacks** on laboratory facilities
- **6-12 hour electricity blackouts** daily
- **Sophisticated cyberattacks** targeting health infrastructure
- **Supply chain disruptions** for diagnostic reagents
- **Staff evacuation** and remote work requirements

Without business continuity planning, this system would have collapsed. Imagine: no HIV viral load testing, no TB drug resistance screening, no COVID diagnostics for a nation of 40 million people during active conflict.

### What We Did

We helped PHC implement ISO 22301 Business Continuity Management:

✓ Disaster Recovery Plans for each laboratory  
✓ Business Continuity Plans with backup sites  
✓ Crisis Management Committee structure  
✓ Communication protocols for emergencies  
✓ Risk assessment and preventive measures  

**Result: 92% operational continuity maintained throughout 2023-2024.**

### The Problem

But here's what kept us awake at night: We paid **$50,000-100,000** to consultants and auditors to help ONE organization. There are:

- **15,000+ HIV/AIDS programs** globally
- **50,000+ health NGOs**
- **Thousands of public health laboratories**

**How many of them can afford this level of support? Almost none.**

## THE BARRIER TO RESILIENCE

The healthcare sector is trapped in a vicious cycle:

### 1. Crisis Exposure
Organizations face increasing threats: pandemics, conflict, climate disasters, cyberattacks, funding cuts

### 2. Knowledge Exists
International standards like ISO 22301, ISO 9001, ISO 27001 provide proven frameworks for resilience

### 3. Access Blocked
Implementation requires $50,000-200,000 in consulting fees, 12-24 months timeline, specialized expertise

### 4. Consultant Dependency
Organizations cannot prepare themselves—they must hire expensive consultants who guide every step

### 5. Audit Monopoly
The same consulting firms that prepare organizations also certify them (conflict of interest)

### 6. Scaling Impossible
Each organization requires dedicated consultant time—helping 10,000 organizations would require 50,000 consultant-years

### The Result

While the Global Fund, PEPFAR, and Gates Foundation spend billions on HIV/AIDS programs, recipient organizations remain institutionally fragile. A cyberattack, funding disruption, or political crisis can destroy years of epidemic response work overnight.

**We're not criticizing consultants—they do excellent work. We're criticizing a system that makes resilience a luxury good instead of a basic right for organizations saving lives.**

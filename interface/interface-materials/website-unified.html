Two Paths When Crisis Hits

Every organization faces this reality. Crises are inevitable—epidemics, disasters, system failures. The question is not IF, but WHEN.

Without Preparedness: Fragile

1

Normal Operations

Services delivered. Patients treated. Community trust established.

2

Crisis Hits

Epidemic | Disaster | Cyber Attack | Supply Disruption

3

No Clear Plan

Confusion, delays, improvisation. Staff overwhelmed without procedures.

4

Services Disrupted

Patients unable to access care. Treatment interrupted. Community loses trust.

5

Severe Consequences

Lives at risk. Long recovery. High costs. Reputation damage.

With BCM / ISO 22301: Resilient

1

Normal Operations

Services delivered. Patients treated. Plans ready, tested.

2

Same Crisis Hits

Same epidemic, disaster—but organization is prepared.

3

Plan Activated Immediately

Trained response. Clear procedures. Coordinated action.

4

Services Maintained

Critical care continues. Patients receive treatment. Staff protected, supported.

5

Rapid Recovery + Learning

Quick return to normal. Lessons captured. Organization stronger.

Organizations have two choices:

1. React when crisis happens → Chaos, improvisation, severe harm
2. Prepare systematically before crisis → Controlled response, continuity, recovery

BCM = Systematic Preparation | ISO 22301 = Proven International Framework

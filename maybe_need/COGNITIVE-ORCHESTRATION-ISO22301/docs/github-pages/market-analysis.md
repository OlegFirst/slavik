  <h2 class="section-title">Critical Market Gaps</h2>

  <div class="gap-analysis">
    <div class="gap-title">Unaddressed Market Needs Creating Massive Opportunity</div>
    <div class="gap-grid">
      <div class="gap-item">
        <h4>AI-Native BCM Platform</h4>
        <p>No existing solution offers true AI-powered business continuity with predictive capabilities and automated decision-making at the core of the platform architecture.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="gap-item">
        <h4>Real-Time Adaptive Planning</h4>
        <p>Current solutions rely on static plans and manual updates. Market lacks dynamic, self-updating BCM plans that adapt to changing business conditions in real-time.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="gap-item">
        <h4>Integrated Digital Ecosystem</h4>
        <p>Fragmented point solutions dominate the market. Organizations need unified platforms that integrate BIA, risk management, incident response, and compliance in one system.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="gap-item">
        <h4>Predictive Risk Intelligence</h4>
        <p>Reactive approaches prevail in current offerings. Market needs proactive solutions that predict disruptions before they occur and automatically trigger preventive measures.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="gap-item">
        <h4>Self-Learning Systems</h4>
        <p>Static knowledge bases are the norm. Industry requires systems that continuously learn from incidents, exercises, and organizational changes to improve effectiveness.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="gap-item">
        <h4>Democratized BCM</h4>
        <p>Current solutions require specialized expertise and extensive training. Market needs intuitive platforms that make advanced BCM accessible to all organizational levels.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

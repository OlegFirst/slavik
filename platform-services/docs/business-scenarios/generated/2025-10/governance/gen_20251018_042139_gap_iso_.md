Title: Management Review Dashboard & Action Tracking
Service: Governance
Category: Reports

**Description**:
The Management Review Dashboard provides comprehensive visibility into the BCMS performance metrics, compliance status, and improvement opportunities for senior leadership. This automated dashboard consolidates key performance indicators, audit findings, incident statistics, and risk trends to support data-driven decision-making during management reviews.

The system automatically generates management review packages according to predefined schedules, tracks action items from previous reviews, and monitors the implementation progress of management decisions. This ensures that management reviews are conducted systematically with consistent inputs and that outcomes are properly documented and followed through.

**Inputs**:
- Review schedule configuration
- KPI thresholds and targets
- Previous review action items
- Performance data selection criteria
- Management review agenda template
- Stakeholder distribution list

**Outputs**:
- Management review dashboard
- Performance trend analysis reports
- Compliance status summary
- Action item tracking report
- Meeting minutes template
- Decision log with assignments

**Events Triggered**:
- management.review.scheduled
- review.package.generated
- action.items.assigned
- review.completed
- follow.up.reminder.sent

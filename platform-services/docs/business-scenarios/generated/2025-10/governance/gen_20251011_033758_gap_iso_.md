Title: Management Review Dashboard and Action Tracking
Service: Governance
Category: Reports

**Description**:
The Management Review Dashboard provides executives and BCM leadership with a comprehensive view of the business continuity management system's performance, compliance status, and improvement opportunities. This centralized platform consolidates key performance indicators, audit findings, incident reports, and stakeholder feedback to facilitate data-driven decision-making during management reviews.

The system automatically generates management review packages according to predefined schedules, tracks action items from previous reviews, and monitors the implementation of management decisions. Real-time dashboards enable leadership to assess BCMS effectiveness, identify trends, and ensure continuous improvement initiatives align with organizational objectives and ISO 22301 requirements.

**Inputs**:
- Review schedule and frequency settings
- Performance metrics and KPI thresholds
- Audit reports and findings
- Incident and crisis response data
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Resource utilization reports
- Previous review action items
- Compliance assessment results

**Outputs**:
- Management review agenda and package
- Performance trend analysis reports
- Compliance status dashboard
- Action item tracking matrix
- Decision documentation
- Improvement opportunity register
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Executive summary presentations

Title: Management Review Meeting Scheduling and Execution
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The BCM Coordinator initiates the quarterly management review process by creating a comprehensive review package that includes performance metrics, audit findings, and improvement opportunities. The system automatically compiles data from various BCM activities including exercise results, incident responses, and compliance assessments to provide management with a complete picture of the BCMS effectiveness.

The management review meeting follows a structured agenda covering BCMS performance, changes in internal and external issues, adequacy of resources, and opportunities for continual improvement. All decisions and action items from the review are documented and tracked through to completion, ensuring that management's strategic direction is effectively implemented throughout the organization.

**Inputs**:
- Review period (start/end dates)
- Previous review action items status
- Performance metrics criteria
- Stakeholder list for review meeting
- Agenda template selection

**Outputs**:
- Management review report
- Performance dashboard
- Action items register
- Resource allocation recommendations
- BCMS improvement plan
- Meeting minutes and attendance record

Title: Management Review Performance Evaluation
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization conducts scheduled management reviews to evaluate the performance and effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). Senior leadership assesses whether the BCMS continues to be suitable, adequate, and effective in achieving business continuity objectives. The review process examines performance metrics, audit results, corrective actions, and changing circumstances that may impact business continuity.

This systematic evaluation ensures that top management maintains oversight of the BCMS performance and makes informed decisions about necessary improvements. The review process includes analysis of key performance indicators, incident response effectiveness, exercise results, and stakeholder feedback to determine if the BCMS aligns with strategic direction and delivers expected outcomes.

**Inputs**:
- Internal audit reports
- Performance metrics and KPIs
- Incident response records
- Exercise and test results
- Corrective action status
- Risk assessment updates
- Stakeholder feedback
- Resource utilization reports
- Previous management review actions

**Outputs**:
- Management review report
- Improvement recommendations
- Resource allocation decisions
- Updated BCMS objectives
- Action items with assigned owners
- Performance trend analysis
- Strategic alignment assessment

Title: Management Review Meeting Scheduling and Execution
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The BCM Coordinator initiates the management review process by scheduling periodic reviews of the business continuity management system (BCMS) performance. The system automatically compiles relevant performance data, audit findings, and improvement opportunities from across all BCM services. This ensures that top management receives comprehensive information to evaluate the continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the BCMS.

The scenario demonstrates compliance by establishing a systematic approach to management reviews, ensuring all required inputs are collected and analyzed. The system tracks review outcomes, decisions, and actions, maintaining evidence of management's active involvement in BCMS governance. Automated notifications and dashboards provide visibility into review schedules, pending actions, and performance trends.

**Inputs**:
- Review frequency and schedule parameters
- Performance metrics and KPIs from all BCM services
- Internal audit results and findings
- External audit reports and certifications
- Incident response performance data
- Training completion statistics
- Resource utilization reports
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Risk assessment updates
- Compliance status reports

**Outputs**:
- Management review agenda and pre-read materials
- Consolidated performance dashboard
- Trend analysis reports
- Action items and decisions register
- Meeting minutes and attendance records
- Updated BCMS objectives and targets
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Improvement plan with assigned responsibilities

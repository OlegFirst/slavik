Title: Management Review Performance Evaluation
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Management Review Performance Evaluation scenario enables organizations to conduct systematic reviews of their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) at planned intervals. This process ensures that the BCMS remains suitable, adequate, and effective while identifying opportunities for improvement and necessary changes to meet evolving business needs and compliance requirements.

The scenario automates the collection and analysis of BCMS performance data, including audit results, incident responses, exercise outcomes, and stakeholder feedback. It generates comprehensive management review packages that provide leadership with actionable insights for strategic decision-making. The system tracks review actions, monitors implementation progress, and ensures that management decisions are effectively communicated and executed throughout the organization.

This systematic approach to management review ensures compliance with ISO 22301 requirements by providing documented evidence of leadership engagement, performance evaluation, and continual improvement. It demonstrates that top management actively reviews the BCMS effectiveness and makes informed decisions based on comprehensive performance data.

**Inputs**:
- Review period and scope definition
- BCMS performance metrics and KPIs
- Internal audit findings and recommendations
- Exercise and test results
- Incident response effectiveness data
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Resource utilization reports
- Previous review actions status

**Outputs**:
- Management review agenda and schedule
- Performance evaluation dashboard
- Trend analysis reports
- Improvement opportunity register
- Management decisions and directives
- Action plan with assigned responsibilities
- Review meeting minutes and attendance records
- Communication plan for review outcomes

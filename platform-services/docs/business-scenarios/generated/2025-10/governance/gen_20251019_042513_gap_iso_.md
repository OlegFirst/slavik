Title: Management Review Meeting Scheduling and Execution
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The BCM Governance module automates the scheduling, preparation, and execution of management review meetings as required by ISO 22301. The system tracks review cycles, automatically compiles performance data, and generates pre-meeting reports covering all aspects of the BCMS performance including audit results, incident responses, and improvement opportunities.

The platform ensures that management reviews are conducted at planned intervals with comprehensive agenda items covering BCMS suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness. It facilitates decision-making by presenting trend analyses, resource utilization metrics, and recommendations for continual improvement. All review outcomes, decisions, and action items are documented and tracked through to completion.

**Inputs**:
- Review frequency settings (quarterly/semi-annual/annual)
- Previous review action items and status
- BCMS performance metrics and KPIs
- Internal audit findings and corrective actions
- Incident response effectiveness data
- Resource allocation and utilization reports
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Changes in external/internal context
- Risk assessment updates
- Training completion statistics

**Outputs**:
- Management review meeting agenda
- Pre-meeting performance dashboard
- Consolidated BCMS effectiveness report
- Meeting minutes and attendance records
- Management decisions and directives
- Action item register with assignments
- Resource allocation approvals
- BCMS improvement plan updates
- Next review schedule confirmation
- Communication plan for review outcomes

**Events Triggered**:

- review.agenda.distributed
- meeting.minutes.recorded
- action.items.assigned
- improvement.plan.updated

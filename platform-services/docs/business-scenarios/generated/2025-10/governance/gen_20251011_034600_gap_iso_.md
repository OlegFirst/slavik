Title: Management Review Meeting Scheduling and Execution
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The BCM Coordinator initiates the management review process by scheduling periodic reviews of the business continuity management system (BCMS) performance. The system automatically compiles relevant performance data, audit results, and improvement opportunities based on predefined intervals and triggers. This ensures that top management regularly evaluates the BCMS effectiveness, resource adequacy, and alignment with strategic direction.

The scenario demonstrates compliance by establishing a systematic approach to management reviews, ensuring all required inputs are collected, analyzed, and presented to leadership. The system tracks review outcomes, decisions, and action items, maintaining evidence of management's active involvement in BCMS governance. Automated notifications and dashboards provide visibility into review schedules, pending items, and follow-up actions.

**Inputs**:
- Review frequency settings (quarterly/semi-annual/annual)
- Previous review action items status
- BCMS performance metrics and KPIs
- Internal audit findings
- External audit results
- Incident reports and lessons learned
- Resource utilization reports
- Stakeholder feedback compilation
- Risk assessment updates
- Compliance status reports

**Outputs**:
- Management review agenda
- Consolidated performance dashboard
- Review meeting minutes
- Decision log with assigned actions
- Resource allocation recommendations
- BCMS improvement plan updates
- Next review schedule
- Compliance evidence package

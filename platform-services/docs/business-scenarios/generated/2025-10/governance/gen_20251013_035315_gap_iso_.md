Title: Management Review Meeting Scheduling and Execution
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The BCM Coordinator initiates the quarterly management review process by creating a comprehensive review package that includes performance metrics, audit findings, and improvement opportunities. The system automatically compiles data from various BCM activities including exercise results, incident responses, and compliance assessments to provide management with a complete picture of the BCMS effectiveness.

The management review meeting follows a structured agenda covering BCMS performance indicators, changes in internal and external issues, nonconformities and corrective actions, and opportunities for continual improvement. The system tracks all review inputs, discussions, decisions, and assigned actions to ensure proper documentation and follow-up of management commitments.

**Inputs**:
- Review period (start and end dates)
- Previous review actions status
- Performance metrics criteria
- Attendee list and roles
- Review agenda template
- Data collection parameters

**Outputs**:
- Management review report
- Performance dashboard
- Action items register
- Resource allocation decisions
- BCMS improvement plan
- Meeting minutes and attendance record

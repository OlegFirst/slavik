Title: Management Review Performance Evaluation
Service: Governance
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Management Review Performance Evaluation scenario enables organizations to conduct comprehensive management reviews of their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) at planned intervals. This systematic evaluation process ensures that the BCMS remains suitable, adequate, and effective in achieving its intended outcomes while identifying opportunities for continual improvement.

The scenario facilitates the collection, analysis, and presentation of key performance data, audit results, and stakeholder feedback to top management. Through automated data aggregation and trend analysis, leadership can make informed decisions about resource allocation, strategic direction, and necessary improvements to the BCMS. The review process includes evaluation of policy effectiveness, objective achievement, and overall system performance against established criteria.

This comprehensive review mechanism ensures compliance with ISO 22301 requirements by providing documented evidence of management's active involvement in BCMS oversight, strategic decision-making, and commitment to continual improvement. The structured approach guarantees that all mandatory review inputs are considered and appropriate outputs are generated and tracked.

**Inputs**:
- Previous management review action items and status
- Internal audit results and findings
- External audit results and certifications
- Performance metrics and KPI data
- Incident response effectiveness reports
- Exercise and test results
- Risk assessment updates
- BIA review outcomes
- Stakeholder feedback and complaints
- Changes in external/internal context
- Resource utilization reports
- Training completion statistics

**Outputs**:
- Management review meeting minutes
- Decision log with assigned actions
- Resource allocation recommendations
- BCMS improvement opportunities
- Policy and objective revision requirements
- Updated strategic direction
- Performance improvement targets
- Corrective action plans
- Communication requirements
- Updated risk appetite statements

Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to systematically identify, analyze, and address risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This framework enables proactive management of potential disruptions while identifying opportunities for BCMS improvement and enhanced organizational resilience.

The system captures both internal and external factors that could affect the organization's ability to achieve its BCMS objectives. Through automated workflows and risk scoring mechanisms, the framework ensures consistent evaluation of risks and opportunities, tracks mitigation actions, and monitors residual risk levels. This approach supports informed decision-making and resource allocation for business continuity initiatives.

**Inputs**:
- Risk/opportunity identification source (internal audit, external assessment, incident analysis)
- Risk category (operational, strategic, compliance, reputational)
- Opportunity type (process improvement, technology enhancement, capability development)
- Impact assessment criteria (financial, operational, reputational, regulatory)
- Likelihood indicators (probability scales, historical data, trend analysis)
- Current control effectiveness ratings
- Resource requirements for treatment options

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register with prioritization scores
- Treatment action plans with assigned responsibilities
- Residual risk assessment reports
- Opportunity realization roadmaps
- Risk heat maps and trend analysis dashboards
- Compliance status against risk appetite thresholds
- Resource allocation recommendations

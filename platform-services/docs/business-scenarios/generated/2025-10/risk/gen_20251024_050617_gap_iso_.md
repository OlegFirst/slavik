Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to systematically identify, analyze, and address risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This framework ensures proactive identification of potential disruptions, vulnerabilities, and improvement opportunities across all business processes and locations.

The assessment process integrates both internal and external factors, including operational risks, strategic opportunities, compliance requirements, and stakeholder expectations. By maintaining a dynamic risk register and opportunity log, the organization can prioritize actions, allocate resources effectively, and enhance overall BCMS resilience while capitalizing on opportunities for continuous improvement.

**Inputs**:
- Business process inventory
- Previous incident reports
- Threat intelligence data
- Stakeholder requirements
- Regulatory compliance obligations
- Business impact analysis results
- External environment factors
- Internal audit findings

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports
- Risk treatment plans
- Opportunity implementation roadmap
- Risk heat maps
- Mitigation strategy documents
- Performance improvement initiatives
- Management review presentations

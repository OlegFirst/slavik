Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to identify, analyze, and address potential threats and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This systematic approach ensures that both internal and external factors are considered when determining risks and opportunities related to business continuity objectives.

The framework integrates with existing organizational risk management processes while maintaining specific focus on business continuity aspects. It enables the organization to proactively identify potential disruptions, assess their likelihood and impact, and determine appropriate actions to either mitigate risks or capitalize on opportunities. This ensures the BCMS remains effective and aligned with the organization's strategic direction.

**Inputs**:
- Internal context factors (organizational structure, culture, capabilities)
- External context factors (regulatory, market, environmental conditions)
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Previous incident reports and lessons learned
- Business impact analysis results
- Compliance obligations and legal requirements

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports with likelihood and impact ratings
- Treatment plans for identified risks
- Opportunity realization strategies
- Risk acceptance criteria documentation
- Monitoring and review schedules

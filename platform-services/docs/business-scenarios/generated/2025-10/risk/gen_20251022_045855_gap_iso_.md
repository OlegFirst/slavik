Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to systematically identify, analyze, and address risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS) and its ability to achieve intended outcomes. This framework ensures proactive identification of potential disruptions, vulnerabilities, and improvement opportunities across all business processes and locations.

The assessment process integrates both internal and external factors, including changes in business environment, regulatory requirements, technology landscape, and stakeholder expectations. By maintaining a dynamic risk register with regular reviews and updates, the organization can prioritize actions to prevent or reduce undesired effects while enhancing positive outcomes for business continuity.

**Inputs**:
- Business context and environmental factors
- Previous incident reports and lessons learned
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Legal and regulatory changes
- Technology and infrastructure dependencies
- Supply chain vulnerabilities
- Resource availability and constraints

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports with impact and likelihood ratings
- Treatment plans with assigned responsibilities
- Residual risk acceptance documentation
- Opportunity implementation roadmap
- Management review presentations

Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment for BCMS Planning
Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
An organization implements a systematic approach to identify and assess risks and opportunities that could impact the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) objectives. The scenario involves conducting quarterly risk assessments across all business units, evaluating both internal and external factors that may affect business continuity capabilities.

The assessment process includes analyzing potential disruptions, regulatory changes, technological vulnerabilities, and opportunities for improvement. Risk owners collaborate with the BCM team to evaluate likelihood and impact ratings, develop treatment plans, and monitor emerging threats. This proactive approach ensures the organization can adapt its BCMS to changing circumstances while maintaining compliance and operational resilience.

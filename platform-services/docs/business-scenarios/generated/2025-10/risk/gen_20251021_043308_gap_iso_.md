Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to identify, analyze, and address potential threats and opportunities that could impact the achievement of business continuity objectives. This systematic approach ensures that both internal and external factors are considered when determining risks and opportunities related to the BCMS scope and strategic direction.

The framework integrates with existing organizational risk management processes while maintaining specific focus on business continuity implications. It enables proactive identification of changing circumstances, emerging threats, and potential improvements to enhance organizational resilience. Regular assessments are conducted to ensure the BCMS remains effective and aligned with organizational objectives.

**Inputs**:
- Organizational context and strategic objectives
- BCMS scope and boundaries
- Previous risk assessment results
- Internal and external issue registers
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Compliance obligations
- Historical incident data
- Business impact analysis results

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports
- Treatment action plans
- Risk acceptance documentation
- Opportunity implementation roadmap
- Executive dashboard metrics
- Compliance status updates

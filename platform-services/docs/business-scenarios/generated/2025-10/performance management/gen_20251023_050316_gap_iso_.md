Title: Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Framework
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario implements a comprehensive performance monitoring and evaluation system that tracks, measures, and analyzes the effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). The framework establishes key performance indicators (KPIs), collects performance data from various BCM activities, and generates insights for continual improvement.

The system automatically monitors critical BCM metrics including recovery time objectives (RTO) achievement rates, business impact analysis completion status, exercise participation rates, and incident response times. Performance data is collected from multiple sources including BIA assessments, risk registers, exercise reports, and incident logs, providing a holistic view of BCMS effectiveness.

Regular performance evaluation cycles ensure that the organization can identify trends, detect performance gaps, and implement corrective actions promptly. This systematic approach to performance monitoring demonstrates compliance with ISO 22301's requirements for measuring BCMS performance and effectiveness.

**Inputs**:
- KPI definitions and targets
- BIA completion metrics
- Risk assessment scores
- Exercise participation data
- Incident response times
- Training completion rates
- Audit findings
- Recovery test results
- Resource utilization data
- Stakeholder feedback scores

**Outputs**:
- Performance dashboard
- KPI achievement reports
- Trend analysis charts
- Performance gap analysis
- Improvement recommendations
- Executive performance summary
- Compliance scorecards
- Benchmarking reports

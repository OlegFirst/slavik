Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario implements a comprehensive performance monitoring and measurement system for the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). The framework establishes systematic processes to monitor, measure, analyze, and evaluate the performance and effectiveness of business continuity activities, ensuring continuous improvement and compliance with organizational objectives.

The system captures key performance indicators (KPIs) across all BCM processes including BIA completion rates, risk assessment coverage, plan testing frequency, incident response times, and recovery objective achievements. Real-time dashboards provide visibility into BCMS performance metrics, enabling management to make data-driven decisions and identify areas requiring improvement or corrective action.

**Inputs**:
- Performance metrics configuration
- KPI thresholds and targets
- Measurement frequency settings
- Data collection sources
- Evaluation criteria
- Reporting requirements

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement reports
- KPI dashboard visualizations
- Trend analysis results
- Compliance scorecards
- Performance evaluation summaries
- Improvement recommendations

Title: Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario implements a comprehensive performance monitoring system that tracks, measures, and evaluates the effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). The framework establishes key performance indicators (KPIs) aligned with business continuity objectives, enabling organizations to assess whether their BCMS is delivering expected outcomes and meeting stakeholder requirements.

The system automatically collects performance data from various BCM activities including exercise results, incident response times, recovery time achievements, and training completion rates. This data is analyzed against predetermined targets and thresholds to identify trends, gaps, and opportunities for improvement. Regular performance reports are generated for management review, ensuring data-driven decision-making and continuous improvement of the BCMS.

**Inputs**:
- BCM objectives and targets
- Performance indicators definition
- Measurement frequency settings
- Data collection sources configuration
- Baseline performance values
- Stakeholder requirements
- Compliance obligations

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement dashboard
- KPI achievement reports
- Trend analysis charts
- Performance gap analysis
- Improvement recommendations
- Management review reports
- Compliance status indicators

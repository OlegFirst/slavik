Title: Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Dashboard
Service: Performance Management
Category: Reports

**Description**:
The Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Dashboard provides real-time visibility into the effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) through comprehensive metrics tracking and analysis. This scenario demonstrates how organizations can systematically monitor, measure, analyze, and evaluate their BCM performance against defined objectives and compliance requirements.

The dashboard aggregates data from multiple BCM activities including exercise results, incident response times, recovery time achievements, and system availability metrics. By providing automated performance evaluation against predetermined KPIs and thresholds, the system enables management to make data-driven decisions for continual improvement. The solution includes trend analysis capabilities that identify performance patterns and potential areas of concern before they impact business continuity capabilities.

**Inputs**:
- BCM exercise completion data and results
- Incident response metrics and timelines
- Recovery time objective (RTO) achievement records
- Recovery point objective (RPO) compliance data
- System availability and uptime statistics
- Training completion rates and competency scores
- Audit findings and non-conformity records
- Resource utilization metrics
- Stakeholder feedback scores

**Outputs**:
- Executive performance summary report
- KPI achievement dashboard with traffic light indicators
- Trend analysis charts for key metrics
- Performance deviation alerts
- Comparative analysis against industry benchmarks
- Automated performance evaluation reports
- Improvement opportunity recommendations
- Compliance status overview

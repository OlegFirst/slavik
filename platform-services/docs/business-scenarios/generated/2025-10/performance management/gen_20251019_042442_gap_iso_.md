Title: Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario implements a comprehensive performance monitoring system that tracks and measures the effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). The system collects key performance indicators (KPIs) from various BCM activities including BIA completion rates, risk assessment updates, exercise participation, and incident response times. Real-time dashboards provide visibility into BCMS performance against established targets and objectives.

The framework enables organizations to demonstrate continuous improvement by identifying performance gaps, tracking corrective actions, and measuring the impact of improvements over time. Automated data collection and analysis reduce manual effort while ensuring consistent and reliable performance measurement across all BCM processes.

**Inputs**:
- Performance objectives and targets
- KPI definitions and measurement criteria
- Data collection frequency settings
- Threshold values for alerts
- Reporting period configurations
- Stakeholder notification preferences

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement dashboard
- KPI trend analysis reports
- Compliance scorecards
- Performance gap analysis
- Improvement opportunity recommendations
- Executive performance summaries

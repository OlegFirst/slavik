Title: Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Framework
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario implements a comprehensive performance monitoring and evaluation system that tracks, measures, and analyzes the effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). The framework establishes key performance indicators (KPIs), collects performance data from various BCM activities, and generates insights for management review and continuous improvement.

The system automatically monitors critical BCM metrics including recovery time objectives (RTO) achievement rates, business impact analysis completion status, exercise participation rates, and incident response times. Performance data is collected from multiple sources including BIA assessments, risk registers, exercise reports, and incident logs, providing a holistic view of BCMS effectiveness.

Regular performance evaluation reports are generated to identify trends, gaps, and improvement opportunities. The framework supports evidence-based decision making by providing quantitative and qualitative performance metrics that demonstrate BCMS maturity and compliance levels to stakeholders and auditors.

**Inputs**:
- KPI definitions and targets
- BIA completion metrics
- Risk assessment scores
- Exercise performance data
- Incident response times
- Training completion rates
- Audit findings
- Corrective action status

**Outputs**:

- Management review reports

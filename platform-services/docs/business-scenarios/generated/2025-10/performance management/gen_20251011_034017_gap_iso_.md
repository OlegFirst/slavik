Title: Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework Implementation
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive performance monitoring and measurement framework to evaluate the effectiveness of its Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This framework establishes key performance indicators (KPIs), measurement criteria, and monitoring processes to ensure the BCMS meets its intended outcomes and continuously improves over time.

The system captures both quantitative and qualitative performance data across all BCMS processes, including incident response times, recovery objectives achievement, training completion rates, and exercise effectiveness. Regular performance reviews enable management to identify trends, gaps, and opportunities for improvement, ensuring the BCMS remains aligned with organizational objectives and stakeholder requirements.

**Inputs**:
- BCMS objectives and targets
- Performance measurement criteria
- KPI definitions and thresholds
- Monitoring frequency requirements
- Data collection methods
- Historical performance data
- Stakeholder performance expectations

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement dashboard
- KPI tracking reports
- Trend analysis reports
- Performance deviation alerts
- Improvement recommendations
- Compliance status indicators
- Management review inputs

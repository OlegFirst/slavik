Title: Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework Implementation
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
A multinational manufacturing company implements a comprehensive performance monitoring framework to track and measure the effectiveness of their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). The organization establishes key performance indicators (KPIs) for critical business processes, recovery time objectives, and resource availability to ensure continuous improvement and compliance with ISO 22301 requirements.

The framework includes automated data collection from various BCM components, real-time dashboards for management visibility, and scheduled performance reviews. This systematic approach enables the organization to identify trends, detect potential issues before they impact business continuity capabilities, and make data-driven decisions for BCMS improvements. Regular performance analysis ensures that the BCMS remains aligned with business objectives and can effectively respond to disruptions.

**Inputs**:
- BCM objectives and targets
- Critical process recovery metrics
- Exercise and test results data
- Incident response times
- Resource utilization statistics
- Training completion rates
- Audit findings and observations
- Stakeholder feedback scores

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement dashboard
- KPI trend analysis reports
- Performance deviation alerts
- Improvement opportunity register
- Management review presentations
- Compliance scorecards
- Performance baseline documentation

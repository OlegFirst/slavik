Title: Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Dashboard
Service: Performance Management
Category: Reports

**Description**:
The Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Dashboard provides real-time visibility into the effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) through comprehensive metrics and KPIs. This scenario enables organizations to continuously monitor, measure, and evaluate their BCM performance against established objectives and targets, ensuring alignment with ISO 22301 requirements for performance evaluation.

The dashboard aggregates data from multiple BCM processes including BIA completion rates, risk assessment updates, exercise performance metrics, and incident response times. By providing automated analysis and trend identification, the system enables management to make data-driven decisions for continual improvement of the BCMS. The visual representation of performance data facilitates quick identification of areas requiring attention and supports evidence-based reporting to top management.

**Inputs**:
- BCM objectives and performance targets
- BIA completion metrics
- Risk assessment update frequencies
- Exercise and test results
- Incident response time data
- Recovery time objective (RTO) achievement rates
- Recovery point objective (RPO) compliance data
- Training completion statistics
- Audit findings and corrective actions
- Resource utilization metrics

**Outputs**:
- Performance evaluation report
- KPI dashboard with trend analysis
- Compliance scorecard
- Performance gap analysis
- Improvement recommendations
- Executive summary report
- Historical performance comparison
- Predictive performance indicators

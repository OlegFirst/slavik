Service: Performance Management

**Description**:
The Performance Monitoring and Evaluation Dashboard provides comprehensive oversight of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) performance against established objectives and key performance indicators. This scenario enables organizations to systematically monitor, measure, analyze, and evaluate BCMS performance through automated data collection, real-time analytics, and customizable reporting mechanisms.

The dashboard aggregates performance data from multiple BCM components including incident response times, recovery objective achievements, exercise completion rates, and audit findings. By providing visual representations of performance trends, compliance metrics, and improvement opportunities, organizations can make data-driven decisions to enhance their BCMS effectiveness and demonstrate continuous improvement to stakeholders and auditors.

**Inputs**:
- Performance metrics configuration (KPIs, targets, thresholds)
- Data collection frequency settings
- Reporting period parameters
- Stakeholder notification preferences
- Performance baseline values
- Measurement criteria definitions

**Outputs**:
- Real-time performance dashboard
- Performance trend analysis reports
- KPI achievement scorecards
- Non-conformance alerts
- Performance evaluation summaries
- Improvement recommendation reports

Title: Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework
Service: Performance Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Performance Monitoring and Measurement Framework establishes systematic processes for monitoring, measuring, analyzing, and evaluating the business continuity management system's performance. This scenario implements comprehensive performance indicators, measurement criteria, and evaluation methods to ensure the BCMS achieves its intended outcomes and continuously improves its effectiveness.

The framework enables organizations to track critical performance metrics across all BCM activities, from risk assessments and BIA updates to exercise completion rates and incident response times. By implementing automated data collection, real-time dashboards, and trend analysis capabilities, organizations can make data-driven decisions about their BCM program effectiveness and identify areas requiring improvement or additional resources.

**Inputs**:
- Performance indicator definitions
- Measurement frequency requirements
- Data collection methods
- Baseline performance values
- Target performance thresholds
- Evaluation criteria
- Reporting requirements
- Stakeholder notification rules

**Outputs**:
- Performance measurement dashboard
- KPI trend analysis reports
- Performance evaluation summaries
- Non-conformance notifications
- Improvement opportunity recommendations
- Compliance status indicators
- Executive performance reports

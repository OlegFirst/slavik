**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic documentation and validation of business impact analysis requirements in accordance with ISO 22301 Clause 8.2. The organization conducts a comprehensive review of all critical business functions, their dependencies, and recovery requirements to establish documented criteria for business continuity planning.

The process involves gathering requirements from all business units, documenting critical activities, resources, and dependencies, and establishing recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs). This ensures that the organization has clearly defined and documented requirements for maintaining business continuity capabilities that align with stakeholder expectations and regulatory obligations.

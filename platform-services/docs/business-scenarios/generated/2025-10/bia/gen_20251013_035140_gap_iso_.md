Title: Business Impact Analysis Strategy Development
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario enables organizations to develop and maintain a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) strategy aligned with ISO 22301 requirements. The system guides users through establishing BIA criteria, defining impact categories, and setting recovery objectives based on organizational context and risk appetite.

The BIA strategy development process ensures systematic identification of critical activities, their dependencies, and resource requirements. It provides a structured approach to determine Maximum Acceptable Outage (MAO) periods and Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) while considering legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations. The system automatically validates completeness of BIA scope against organizational structure and maintains version control for strategy updates.

**Inputs**:
- Organizational structure and business units
- Business activity inventory
- Regulatory and compliance requirements
- Historical incident data
- Stakeholder impact tolerance levels
- Resource availability constraints
- Previous BIA results (if available)

**Outputs**:
- BIA methodology document
- Impact assessment criteria matrix
- Activity criticality classification framework
- RTO/RPO determination guidelines
- BIA schedule and timeline
- Stakeholder communication plan
- BIA scope validation report

**Events Triggered**:

- bia.scope.validated
- bia.schedule.approved

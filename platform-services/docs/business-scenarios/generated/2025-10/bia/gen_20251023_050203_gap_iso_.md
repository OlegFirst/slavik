Title: Business Impact Analysis Execution and Documentation
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic execution of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and analyze the potential impacts of disrupting events on the organization's operations. The process involves collecting data from business units, analyzing dependencies, determining recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO), and documenting critical business functions and their resource requirements.

The BIA execution follows a structured methodology that engages stakeholders across the organization to assess the consequences of disruptions at various time intervals. This comprehensive analysis forms the foundation for developing appropriate business continuity strategies and ensures that recovery priorities align with business objectives and stakeholder expectations.

**Inputs**:
- Business unit information and organizational structure
- Process inventory and workflow documentation
- Historical incident data and impact records
- Resource dependency mappings
- Financial and operational metrics
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations

**Outputs**:
- Critical business function identification report
- Maximum tolerable period of disruption (MTPD) analysis
- Recovery time objectives (RTO) documentation
- Recovery point objectives (RPO) specifications
- Resource requirement matrix
- Dependency mapping visualization
- Impact assessment timeline charts
- Priority ranking of business activities

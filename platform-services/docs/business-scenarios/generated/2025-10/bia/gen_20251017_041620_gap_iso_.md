**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic execution of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and analyze the potential impacts of disrupting events on the organization's operations. The BIA process evaluates critical business functions, their dependencies, and recovery requirements to establish priorities for business continuity planning.

The scenario ensures compliance with ISO 22301 Clause 8.3 by implementing a structured approach to determine recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). This analysis provides the foundation for developing appropriate business continuity strategies and solutions based on the organization's risk appetite and resource constraints.

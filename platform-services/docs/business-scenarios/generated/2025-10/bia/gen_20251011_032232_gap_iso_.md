**Description**:
A multinational manufacturing company needs to establish and document comprehensive requirements for conducting Business Impact Analysis across its global operations. The organization must identify and document all critical business activities, their interdependencies, and resource requirements to ensure effective business continuity planning.

The scenario involves creating a structured framework for capturing BIA requirements including recovery time objectives (RTOs), recovery point objectives (RPOs), minimum business continuity objectives (MBCOs), and critical resource dependencies. This systematic approach ensures that all stakeholder requirements are properly documented, validated, and incorporated into the business continuity management system.

**Inputs**:
- Organizational strategic objectives
- Business process inventory
- Stakeholder identification matrix
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Historical incident data
- Resource dependency mappings
- Customer service level agreements

**Outputs**:
- BIA requirements specification document
- Prioritized critical activities register
- RTO/RPO requirements matrix
- Resource requirements catalog
- Stakeholder requirements validation report
- Gap analysis between current capabilities and requirements

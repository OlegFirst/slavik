Title: Implement Business Impact Analysis and Risk Assessment Framework

Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
A multinational manufacturing company needs to establish a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and risk assessment process to identify critical business activities, their dependencies, and potential disruptions. The organization operates across multiple regions with complex supply chains and must ensure business continuity for its critical operations.

The scenario involves conducting systematic analysis of all business functions to determine recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). This process will identify resource requirements, interdependencies between processes, and prioritize activities based on their impact on the organization's objectives.

**Inputs**:
- Business process inventory
- Organizational structure and departments
- Historical incident data
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Stakeholder requirements
- Current resource allocations
- Technology dependencies mapping

**Outputs**:
- Critical activity identification report
- Recovery time objectives (RTO) matrix
- Recovery point objectives (RPO) documentation
- Resource requirement analysis
- Dependency mapping diagrams
- Risk assessment register
- Priority ranking of business activities

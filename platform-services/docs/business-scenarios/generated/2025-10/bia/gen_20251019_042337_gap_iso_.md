**Description**:
This scenario addresses the development and implementation of a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) strategy aligned with ISO 22301 Clause 8.3 requirements. The process establishes systematic procedures for identifying critical business activities, determining their recovery priorities, and defining resource requirements based on maximum acceptable outage times.

The BIA strategy development process ensures organizations can effectively assess the impacts of disrupting incidents on their operations, products, and services. By implementing structured analysis methodologies, organizations can prioritize recovery efforts, allocate resources efficiently, and establish recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs) that align with business needs and stakeholder expectations.

Service: BIA

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic execution of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and document critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. The organization conducts comprehensive BIA assessments across all business units to determine the impact of disruptions on operations, establishing recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) for each critical activity.

The BIA process involves structured interviews with process owners, analysis of interdependencies between business functions, and assessment of resource requirements for continuity. This systematic approach ensures that the organization understands its critical activities and can prioritize recovery efforts based on documented impact assessments, meeting ISO 22301's requirements for determining business continuity requirements through formal analysis.

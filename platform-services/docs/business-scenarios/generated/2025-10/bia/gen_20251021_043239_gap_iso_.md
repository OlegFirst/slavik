Title: Business Impact Analysis Strategy Development
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) strategy to identify and evaluate the potential impacts of disruptions on critical business activities. This scenario focuses on developing and maintaining a systematic approach to determine recovery priorities, resource requirements, and maximum tolerable periods of disruption (MTPD) for all business functions.

The BIA strategy development process ensures that the organization understands its critical dependencies, establishes clear recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO), and identifies the minimum resources needed to maintain operations at an acceptable level. This systematic approach enables informed decision-making about business continuity investments and ensures that recovery strategies are aligned with actual business needs and stakeholder expectations.

**Outputs**:

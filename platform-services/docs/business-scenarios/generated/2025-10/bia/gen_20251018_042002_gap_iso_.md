Title: Business Impact Analysis Execution and Documentation
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic execution of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and analyze the potential impacts of disrupting events on the organization's operations. The process involves collecting data from business units, analyzing dependencies, determining recovery priorities, and establishing recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs).

The BIA execution process ensures comprehensive understanding of critical business activities, their interdependencies, and resource requirements. This analysis forms the foundation for developing effective business continuity strategies and plans by providing clear priorities based on impact severity and time sensitivity.

**Inputs**:
- Business process inventory
- Organizational structure data
- Historical incident impact data
- Resource dependency mappings
- Financial impact thresholds
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Stakeholder criticality assessments

**Outputs**:
- Critical activity identification report
- Maximum tolerable period of disruption (MTPD) values
- Recovery time objectives (RTO) matrix
- Recovery point objectives (RPO) specifications
- Resource requirement analysis
- Dependency mapping documentation
- Priority ranking of business activities

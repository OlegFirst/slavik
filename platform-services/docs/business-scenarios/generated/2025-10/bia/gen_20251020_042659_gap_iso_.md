Title: Business Impact Analysis Requirements Definition and Validation
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization conducts a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and document critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system captures, validates, and maintains BIA requirements in alignment with organizational objectives and stakeholder expectations.

The BIA requirements definition process ensures that all critical activities are assessed against standardized criteria, including maximum tolerable period of disruption (MTPD), recovery time objectives (RTO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). The system automatically validates these requirements against organizational policies and regulatory constraints, ensuring consistency and compliance across all business units.

**Inputs**:
- Business activity inventory
- Stakeholder requirements matrix
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Historical incident data
- Resource dependency mappings
- Financial impact thresholds
- Operational impact criteria

**Outputs**:
- Validated BIA requirements document
- Critical activity register with prioritization
- Recovery time objectives (RTO) matrix
- Recovery point objectives (RPO) specifications
- Resource requirements catalog
- Dependency mapping reports
- Compliance validation report

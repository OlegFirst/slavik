Title: Business Impact Analysis Strategy Development
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization needs to establish and implement a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) strategy that identifies critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery priorities. This scenario involves developing a structured approach to analyze the potential impacts of disruptions on business operations, including financial, operational, legal, and reputational consequences.

The BIA strategy development process ensures systematic identification of critical activities, their interdependencies, and resource requirements. This enables the organization to determine recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs) based on actual business needs and impact thresholds. The strategy includes defining assessment criteria, impact categories, and escalating consequences over time.

**Inputs**:
- Organizational structure and business units
- Business process inventory
- Historical incident data
- Regulatory and contractual requirements
- Stakeholder impact tolerance levels
- Resource dependency mappings
- Previous BIA results (if available)

**Outputs**:
- BIA methodology document
- Impact assessment criteria and scales
- Critical activity identification matrix
- RTO/RPO determination framework
- BIA schedule and timeline
- Stakeholder engagement plan
- BIA questionnaire templates

**Description**:
A multinational financial services company needs to establish comprehensive requirements for conducting Business Impact Analysis across all critical business functions. The organization must define clear criteria for identifying critical activities, determining Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD), Recovery Time Objectives (RTO), and minimum resource requirements for business continuity.

The scenario involves creating a structured framework that captures all necessary requirements for BIA execution, including stakeholder identification, data collection methods, impact assessment criteria, and prioritization mechanisms. This ensures that the organization can systematically analyze the potential impacts of disruptions on its operations, products, and services while meeting regulatory obligations and stakeholder expectations.

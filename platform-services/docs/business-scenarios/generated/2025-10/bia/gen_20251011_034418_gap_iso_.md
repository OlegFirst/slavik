**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic execution of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and analyze the potential impacts of disrupting events on business operations. The process involves conducting structured assessments with business units to determine critical activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. The BIA execution follows a standardized methodology that ensures comprehensive coverage of all business functions and their interdependencies.

The scenario captures how organizations collect, analyze, and document critical business function data including Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD), Recovery Time Objectives (RTO), and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO). This systematic approach enables the organization to prioritize recovery strategies based on criticality and impact severity. The documented BIA results form the foundation for developing effective business continuity strategies and plans.

**Inputs**:
- Business unit identification and scope
- Process and activity inventory
- Stakeholder contact information
- Historical incident data
- Current recovery capabilities
- Resource dependency mappings
- Financial impact thresholds
- Operational impact criteria

**Outputs**:
- Critical business functions register
- MTPD/RTO/RPO requirements matrix
- Resource dependency analysis report
- Impact severity classifications
- Recovery priority rankings
- Minimum resource requirements
- Single points of failure identification
- BIA summary report with recommendations

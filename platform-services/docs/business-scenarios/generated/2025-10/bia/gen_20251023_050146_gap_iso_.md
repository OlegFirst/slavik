**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic approach to defining and documenting business impact analysis requirements in accordance with ISO 22301 Clause 8.2. The organization establishes clear requirements for conducting BIA, including criteria for impact assessment, recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). The process ensures that all critical business functions are identified and their dependencies are mapped comprehensively.

The scenario involves cross-functional collaboration where business units define their operational requirements, IT defines technical capabilities, and BCM team consolidates these into actionable BIA requirements. This structured approach ensures that the organization's business continuity requirements are clearly documented, validated by stakeholders, and aligned with strategic objectives. The validation process includes verification that requirements are measurable, achievable, and support the organization's risk appetite.

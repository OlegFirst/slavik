**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic documentation and validation of business impact analysis requirements as mandated by ISO 22301 Clause 8.2. The organization conducts a comprehensive BIA to identify and document critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. This process ensures that all business continuity requirements are properly identified, documented, and validated with stakeholders.

The BIA requirements documentation process involves collecting data from business units, analyzing interdependencies, and establishing recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs). This structured approach ensures that the organization has a clear understanding of its critical functions and the resources required to maintain or restore them during disruptions. The validation process includes stakeholder review and approval of identified requirements to ensure accuracy and completeness.

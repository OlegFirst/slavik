Title: Business Impact Analysis Execution and Documentation
Service: BIA
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic execution of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to identify and document critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. The organization conducts comprehensive BIA assessments across all business units to determine the potential impacts of disruptions on operations, establishing recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) for each critical activity.

The BIA process involves structured interviews with process owners, analysis of interdependencies between business functions, and assessment of resource requirements for recovery. This information forms the foundation for developing effective business continuity strategies and ensures that recovery priorities align with organizational objectives and stakeholder expectations.

**Inputs**:
- Business unit identification
- Process inventory list
- Stakeholder contact information
- Historical incident data
- Financial impact thresholds
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Customer service level agreements

**Outputs**:
- Critical activity register
- Maximum tolerable period of disruption (MTPD) for each activity
- Recovery time objectives (RTO)
- Recovery point objectives (RPO)
- Resource requirement documentation
- Dependency mapping reports
- Impact assessment summary

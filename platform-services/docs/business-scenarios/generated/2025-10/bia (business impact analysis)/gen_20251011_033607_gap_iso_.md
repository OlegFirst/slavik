**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive Business Impact Analysis (BIA) process to systematically identify and document critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system captures, validates, and maintains BIA requirements in alignment with ISO 22301 Clause 8.2, ensuring that all critical activities are properly analyzed with defined recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO).

The BIA requirements documentation process includes automated workflows for stakeholder input collection, dependency mapping, and impact assessment. The system enforces mandatory fields for criticality ratings, resource requirements, and recovery priorities while maintaining version control and approval trails. This ensures that business continuity requirements are consistently documented, regularly reviewed, and aligned with organizational objectives.

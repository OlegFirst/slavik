**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic documentation and validation of business impact analysis requirements as mandated by ISO 22301 Clause 8.2. The organization conducts a comprehensive BIA to identify and document critical business activities, their dependencies, and recovery requirements. The process ensures all business continuity requirements are properly captured, validated, and maintained in accordance with the standard.

The BIA requirements documentation process involves stakeholder engagement across all business units to identify critical processes, maximum tolerable periods of disruption (MTPD), recovery time objectives (RTO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). This structured approach ensures that the organization's business continuity management system is built on accurately documented and validated requirements that reflect the true needs of the business.

Service: Risk Management

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the organization's systematic approach to identifying, analyzing, and treating risks that could disrupt critical business operations. The process involves conducting comprehensive risk assessments across all business units, evaluating potential threats and vulnerabilities, and implementing appropriate risk treatment measures in alignment with the organization's risk appetite and business continuity objectives.

The scenario showcases how the organization operationalizes its risk management framework by integrating business impact analysis results with threat assessments to prioritize risks and allocate resources effectively. This ensures that business continuity strategies are based on documented risk assessments and that treatment plans are monitored and updated regularly to maintain their effectiveness.

- Threat catalog and vulnerability assessments
- Risk assessment criteria and methodology
- Historical incident data
- Regulatory and compliance requirements
- Stakeholder risk tolerance levels

**Outputs**:

- Risk treatment plans and timelines
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Risk monitoring dashboards
- Residual risk reports

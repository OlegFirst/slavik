Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive framework for identifying and assessing risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This systematic approach ensures that potential threats to business operations are proactively identified, evaluated, and addressed through appropriate risk treatment measures.

The framework integrates with existing business processes to continuously monitor internal and external factors that may affect the organization's ability to achieve its BCMS objectives. By establishing clear criteria for risk assessment and opportunity identification, the organization can prioritize resources effectively and maintain resilience against disruptions.

**Inputs**:
- Business context and environment analysis
- Previous incident reports and lessons learned
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Legal and regulatory compliance requirements
- Business impact analysis results
- Threat intelligence and vulnerability assessments
- Industry benchmarks and best practices

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment matrix with likelihood and impact ratings
- Risk treatment plans and mitigation strategies
- Opportunity realization plans
- Risk acceptance documentation
- Residual risk reports
- Risk monitoring dashboards

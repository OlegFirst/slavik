Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to systematically identify, analyze, and address risks and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This framework ensures proactive identification of potential disruptions, vulnerabilities, and improvement opportunities across all business processes and locations.

The assessment process integrates both internal and external factors, including operational risks, supply chain dependencies, regulatory changes, technological threats, and emerging opportunities for enhancing resilience. Regular risk assessments are conducted at predetermined intervals and triggered by significant changes in the business environment, ensuring the BCMS remains effective and aligned with organizational objectives.

**Inputs**:
- Business process inventory
- Previous risk assessment results
- Incident and disruption history
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- External threat intelligence
- Regulatory and compliance requirements
- Business impact analysis results
- Resource availability and constraints

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports with severity ratings
- Risk treatment plans and mitigation strategies
- Opportunity implementation roadmaps
- Risk acceptance documentation
- Updated BCMS objectives and targets
- Performance indicators for risk monitoring

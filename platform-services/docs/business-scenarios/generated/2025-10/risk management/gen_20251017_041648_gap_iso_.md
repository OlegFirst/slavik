Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to identify, analyze, and address potential threats and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This systematic approach ensures that both internal and external factors are considered when determining risks and opportunities that need to be addressed to achieve intended outcomes and prevent undesired effects.

The framework integrates with existing business processes to continuously monitor changes in the business environment, regulatory landscape, and stakeholder expectations. By proactively identifying and addressing risks and opportunities, the organization can enhance desirable effects, prevent or reduce undesired impacts, and achieve continual improvement of the BCMS effectiveness.

**Inputs**:
- Internal context factors (organizational structure, policies, objectives)
- External context factors (regulatory, market, environmental conditions)
- Previous risk assessment results
- Incident and disruption history
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- BCMS performance data
- Audit findings and management review outputs

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk treatment plans
- Opportunity implementation plans
- Risk assessment reports
- Priority matrix for risks and opportunities
- Resource allocation recommendations
- BCMS improvement initiatives

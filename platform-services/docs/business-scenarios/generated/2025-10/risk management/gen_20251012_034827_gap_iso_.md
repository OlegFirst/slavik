Title: Risk and Opportunity Assessment Framework

Service: Risk Management
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization implements a comprehensive risk and opportunity assessment framework to identify, analyze, and address potential threats and opportunities that could impact the business continuity management system (BCMS). This systematic approach ensures that both internal and external factors are considered when determining risks and opportunities that need to be addressed to achieve intended outcomes and prevent undesired effects.

The framework integrates with existing business processes to capture risks and opportunities from various sources including business impact analyses, threat assessments, vulnerability reviews, and strategic planning sessions. By maintaining a centralized repository of identified risks and opportunities, the organization can prioritize actions, allocate resources effectively, and ensure continuous improvement of the BCMS while maintaining alignment with strategic objectives.

**Inputs**:
- Business context and environment analysis
- Previous incident reports and lessons learned
- Regulatory and compliance requirements
- Stakeholder concerns and expectations
- Business impact analysis results
- Threat and vulnerability assessments
- Strategic objectives and goals
- Resource availability and constraints

**Outputs**:
- Risk and opportunity register
- Risk assessment reports with likelihood and impact ratings
- Prioritized action plans for risk treatment
- Opportunity realization strategies
- Risk treatment options (avoid, accept, mitigate, transfer)
- Residual risk evaluation
- Performance indicators for risk monitoring
- Management review reports

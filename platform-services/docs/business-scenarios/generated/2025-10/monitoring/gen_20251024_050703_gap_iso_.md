Title: Performance Metrics Dashboard for BCM System Monitoring
Service: Monitoring
Category: Reports

**Description**:
This scenario implements a comprehensive performance monitoring dashboard that tracks and evaluates the effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) against predetermined metrics and indicators. The dashboard provides real-time visibility into critical performance parameters including recovery time objectives (RTO) achievement rates, business impact analysis completion status, and incident response times.

The system automatically collects performance data from various BCM components, analyzes trends, and generates actionable insights for management review. This enables organizations to identify areas requiring improvement, track progress against BCM objectives, and demonstrate continuous improvement in line with ISO 22301 requirements for performance evaluation.

**Inputs**:
- BCM activity logs and timestamps
- Recovery exercise results and timelines
- Incident response metrics
- BIA completion percentages
- Risk assessment update frequencies
- Training completion rates
- Audit findings and corrective actions
- System availability metrics

**Outputs**:
- Real-time performance dashboard
- KPI trend analysis reports
- Performance deviation alerts
- Compliance score calculations
- Executive summary reports
- Improvement recommendation lists

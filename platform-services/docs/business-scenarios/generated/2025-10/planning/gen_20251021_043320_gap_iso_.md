Title: Business Continuity Objectives Definition and Tracking

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains business continuity objectives that are consistent with the business continuity policy and aligned with strategic direction. These objectives are set at relevant functions and levels within the organization, taking into account applicable requirements, risks, and opportunities identified during the business impact analysis and risk assessment processes.

The system enables systematic definition, documentation, and tracking of measurable business continuity objectives. Each objective includes specific targets, timelines, responsible parties, required resources, and evaluation criteria. The platform automatically monitors progress against these objectives and generates alerts when milestones approach or when performance deviates from established targets.

**Inputs**:
- Strategic business goals and priorities
- Business continuity policy requirements
- Risk assessment results
- Business impact analysis findings
- Resource availability constraints
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Historical incident data
- Stakeholder requirements

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC objectives with SMART criteria
- Objective achievement dashboards
- Progress tracking reports
- Resource allocation plans
- Performance indicators and metrics
- Objective review schedules
- Gap analysis reports

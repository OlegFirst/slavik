Title: Implement Business Continuity Strategies and Solutions

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
A multinational manufacturing company needs to establish and implement business continuity strategies and solutions to protect its critical operations from disruptions. The organization must develop specific strategies for each critical activity identified in the BIA, ensuring that recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs) can be met during various disruption scenarios.

The implementation involves selecting appropriate risk treatment options, developing resource requirements, and establishing detailed solutions that address people, premises, technology, information, and suppliers. Each strategy must be cost-effective, feasible, and aligned with the organization's risk appetite while ensuring minimal acceptable service levels can be maintained during disruptions.

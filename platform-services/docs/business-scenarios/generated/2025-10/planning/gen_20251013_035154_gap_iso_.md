Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and Risk Assessment. Organizations must identify and evaluate multiple strategy options for protecting prioritized activities and their supporting resources, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery objectives.

The process involves analyzing resource requirements, identifying strategy options for each critical activity, evaluating alternatives against defined criteria, and selecting optimal strategies that meet recovery time objectives while considering organizational constraints. This ensures that the organization has predetermined, viable approaches for maintaining and recovering critical operations during disruptions.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results (critical activities, RTOs, RPOs, dependencies)
- Risk assessment outputs (threat scenarios, vulnerability assessments)
- Resource requirements (people, technology, facilities, suppliers)
- Financial constraints and budget parameters
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Existing capability assessments

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategies for each critical activity
- Strategy selection criteria and evaluation matrix
- Cost-benefit analysis for selected strategies
- Resource allocation plans
- Implementation roadmap with timelines
- Strategy approval records

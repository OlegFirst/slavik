Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of business impact analysis and risk assessment. Organizations must identify and evaluate multiple strategy options for protecting prioritized activities and their supporting resources, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery objectives.

The process involves analyzing different strategic approaches for maintaining critical operations during disruptions, including alternative locations, technology solutions, workforce arrangements, and supply chain alternatives. Each strategy option is evaluated against predefined criteria to ensure it can meet the established recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) while remaining practical and financially viable.

Strategy selection requires collaboration between business units, IT, facilities, and senior management to ensure chosen strategies are comprehensive, integrated, and supported across the organization. The selected strategies form the foundation for developing detailed business continuity plans and procedures.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results with prioritized activities and RTOs/RPOs
- Risk assessment findings and impact scenarios
- Resource requirements for critical activities
- Current capability assessment
- Budget constraints and financial parameters
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategy options with cost-benefit analysis
- Selected strategies for each critical activity
- Resource allocation plan for strategy implementation
- Strategy approval documentation
- Implementation roadmap and timeline
- Strategy effectiveness criteria and metrics

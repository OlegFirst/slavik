Title: Define and Track Business Continuity Objectives and Performance Targets
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
An organization establishes measurable business continuity objectives aligned with its strategic direction and business continuity policy. The scenario involves setting specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) objectives for critical business functions identified during the BIA process. These objectives include recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO) for each critical process.

The organization implements a systematic approach to track progress against these objectives through regular monitoring and measurement. Performance indicators are established for each objective, with clear accountability assigned to process owners. The objectives are reviewed quarterly to ensure they remain relevant to changing business needs and risk landscape.

This scenario demonstrates compliance by ensuring business continuity objectives are formally documented, communicated to relevant stakeholders, and integrated into the organization's performance management framework. The objectives directly support the achievement of the business continuity policy and are consistent with applicable requirements.

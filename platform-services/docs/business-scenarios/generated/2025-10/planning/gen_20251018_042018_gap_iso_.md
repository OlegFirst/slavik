Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and Risk Assessment. Organizations must identify and evaluate multiple strategy options for protecting prioritized activities and their dependencies, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery objectives.

The process involves analyzing different strategic approaches for continuity, including risk mitigation, resource redundancy, alternative processes, and third-party arrangements. Each strategy option is evaluated against predefined criteria to ensure it can meet the established Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs) while remaining practical and sustainable for the organization.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results with prioritized activities and dependencies
- Risk assessment outcomes and treatment priorities
- Resource requirements and constraints
- Recovery time and point objectives (RTO/RPO)
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Budget and cost constraints
- Regulatory and contractual obligations

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategy options with cost-benefit analysis
- Selected strategies for each critical activity
- Resource allocation plan
- Implementation roadmap and timelines
- Strategy approval documentation
- Gap analysis between current and target capabilities

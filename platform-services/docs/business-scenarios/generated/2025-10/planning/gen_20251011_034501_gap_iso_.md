Title: Business Continuity Objectives Setting and Review

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains business continuity objectives that are consistent with the business continuity policy and aligned with the organization's strategic direction. These objectives are set at relevant functions and levels within the organization, taking into account the results of business impact analysis and risk assessment.

The system enables systematic definition, tracking, and review of measurable business continuity objectives including recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objectives (MBCO). Each objective is linked to specific business processes, assigned to responsible parties, and monitored through key performance indicators to ensure continuous improvement of the BCMS.

**Inputs**:
- Business continuity policy requirements
- BIA results (critical processes, dependencies)
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Stakeholder requirements
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Previous performance data
- Resource availability constraints

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC objectives matrix
- Objective achievement metrics
- Performance dashboards
- Objective review reports
- Gap analysis results
- Improvement action plans

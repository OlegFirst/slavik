Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of business impact analysis and risk assessment. Organizations must identify and evaluate multiple strategic options for protecting prioritized activities and their supporting resources, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery objectives.

The process involves analyzing different approaches to maintain critical operations during disruptions, including alternative work locations, technology solutions, resource redundancy, and third-party arrangements. Each strategy option is evaluated against predefined criteria to ensure it can meet the established recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) while remaining practical and financially viable for the organization.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results with prioritized activities and RTOs/RPOs
- Risk assessment findings and impact scenarios
- Resource requirements for critical activities
- Current capability assessment
- Budget constraints and financial parameters
- Regulatory and contractual obligations

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategy options with cost-benefit analysis
- Selected strategies for each critical activity
- Resource allocation plan
- Implementation roadmap
- Strategy approval documentation
- Gap analysis between current and target capabilities

Title: Business Continuity Procedures Documentation and Version Control
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish, implement, and maintain documented procedures that enable effective response to disruptive incidents. This scenario addresses the systematic documentation, version control, and accessibility of business continuity procedures across the organization. The system ensures all procedures are current, approved, and readily available to relevant personnel during both normal operations and crisis situations.

The automated procedure management system tracks all business continuity procedures including incident response protocols, crisis communication plans, recovery procedures, and escalation matrices. Each procedure undergoes formal review and approval workflows, with version history maintained for audit purposes. The system ensures procedures are aligned with identified risks, recovery objectives, and resource requirements established during BIA and risk assessment processes.

**Inputs**:
- Procedure document templates
- Recovery time objectives (RTO/RPO)
- Resource requirements and dependencies
- Approval authority matrix
- Review cycle parameters
- Access control permissions
- Procedure categorization criteria

**Outputs**:
- Approved BC procedures library
- Version control history
- Procedure accessibility reports
- Review and update schedules
- Training requirement notifications
- Procedure effectiveness metrics
- Compliance audit trails

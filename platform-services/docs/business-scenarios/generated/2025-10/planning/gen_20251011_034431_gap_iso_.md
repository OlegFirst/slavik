Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of the Business Impact Analysis (BIA) and Risk Assessment. Organizations must identify and evaluate multiple strategy options for protecting prioritized activities, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery time objectives.

The process involves analyzing resource requirements, evaluating alternative approaches, and selecting optimal strategies that balance risk reduction with organizational constraints. Strategy selection must consider dependencies between activities, available resources, and the organization's risk appetite while ensuring strategies can achieve the required recovery objectives identified during the BIA process.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results (RTOs, RPOs, resource requirements)
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Resource availability constraints
- Budget parameters
- Regulatory requirements
- Stakeholder requirements
- Current capability assessment

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategies for each critical activity
- Strategy selection rationale
- Resource allocation plan
- Implementation roadmap
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Gap analysis report

Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of business impact analysis and risk assessment. The organization evaluates multiple strategic options for protecting prioritized activities and their supporting resources, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery objectives.

The process ensures that selected strategies address the protection of people, premises, technology, information, supplies, and stakeholders. Each strategy is evaluated against defined criteria including implementation timeframe, resource requirements, and ability to meet established recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs).

**Inputs**:
- BIA results with prioritized activities and RTOs/RPOs
- Risk assessment outcomes and treatment priorities
- Resource dependency mapping
- Budget constraints and available resources
- Regulatory and contractual requirements
- Stakeholder expectations and requirements

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategies for each critical activity
- Strategy selection criteria and evaluation matrix
- Resource requirements for strategy implementation
- Cost-benefit analysis for selected strategies
- Implementation roadmap with timelines
- Strategy approval records and sign-offs

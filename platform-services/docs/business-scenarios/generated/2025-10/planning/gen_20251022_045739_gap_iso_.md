Title: Business Continuity Planning and Operational Control Implementation

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the establishment and implementation of operational planning and control processes required for effective business continuity management. Organizations must plan, implement, and control the processes needed to meet BCMS requirements and implement actions determined during risk assessment and business impact analysis.

The scenario covers the creation of documented procedures for critical business processes, establishment of control criteria, implementation of process controls, and maintenance of documented information. This ensures that planned arrangements are suitable for achieving intended outcomes and that any unintended changes are reviewed and controlled to mitigate adverse effects.

Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of business impact analysis and risk assessment. Organizations must identify and evaluate multiple strategy options for protecting prioritized activities and their supporting resources, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery time objectives.

The process ensures that selected strategies address the protection and continuity of key resources including people, information, technology, premises, and suppliers. Each strategy must be evaluated against defined criteria including implementation timeframe, resource requirements, and potential impact on other business operations. The selection process involves relevant stakeholders and results in documented strategies that form the foundation for detailed business continuity plans.

**Inputs**:
- Business Impact Analysis results
- Risk Assessment findings
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- Resource dependencies mapping
- Budget constraints
- Regulatory requirements
- Stakeholder requirements

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategies for each critical activity
- Strategy evaluation matrix
- Cost-benefit analysis report
- Resource requirement specifications
- Implementation roadmap
- Strategy approval records
- Gap analysis report

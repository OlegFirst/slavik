Title: Operational Planning and Control Implementation

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish, implement, and maintain processes needed to meet business continuity requirements and implement actions determined during planning activities. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system enables systematic operational planning and control by providing structured workflows for developing, reviewing, and maintaining business continuity procedures, work instructions, and operational controls.

The system facilitates the creation of operational procedures that define how business continuity activities will be performed, including clear assignment of responsibilities, resource requirements, and performance criteria. Through automated workflow management and version control, organizations can ensure that operational planning activities are consistently executed, documented, and aligned with the overall BCMS objectives. This approach enables organizations to maintain operational control over their business continuity processes while ensuring that planned activities are implemented effectively.

- Performance criteria and metrics

**Outputs**:

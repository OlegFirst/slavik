Title: Business Continuity Objectives Setting and Review

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains business continuity objectives that are consistent with the business continuity policy and aligned with the organization's strategic direction. These objectives are set at relevant functions and levels within the organization, taking into account the results of business impact analysis and risk assessment.

The system enables systematic definition, documentation, and monitoring of measurable business continuity objectives. Each objective includes specific targets for recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO), and minimum business continuity objective (MBCO) for critical activities. The platform ensures objectives are communicated to relevant stakeholders and reviewed at planned intervals to ensure continued suitability and effectiveness.

**Inputs**:
- Business continuity policy requirements
- BIA results (critical activities, dependencies, priorities)
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Stakeholder requirements
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Previous performance data
- Resource availability constraints

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC objectives with measurable targets
- Objective achievement plans with timelines
- Resource allocation requirements
- Performance indicators for each objective
- Objective review schedule
- Stakeholder communication matrix

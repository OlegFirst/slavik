Title: Business Continuity Planning and Operational Controls Implementation

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the establishment and implementation of operational planning and controls necessary for meeting business continuity requirements. Organizations must plan, implement, and control the processes needed to meet requirements and implement actions determined during planning activities. This includes establishing criteria for processes, implementing control measures, and maintaining documented information to ensure confidence that processes are carried out as planned.

The scenario covers the systematic approach to operational planning, including resource determination, process control criteria, and the management of planned changes. It ensures that outsourced processes affecting business continuity are identified and controlled, while maintaining the necessary documented information to demonstrate that processes have been carried out as planned and meet the organization's business continuity objectives.

**Inputs**:

- Process performance criteria

- Stakeholder requirements and expectations

**Outputs**:

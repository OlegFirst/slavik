Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the organization's systematic approach to developing and selecting appropriate business continuity strategies based on the results of business impact analysis and risk assessment. The process ensures that strategies are aligned with recovery objectives, resource requirements, and organizational capabilities while considering cost-effectiveness and feasibility.

The strategy development workflow incorporates multiple stakeholder inputs, evaluates various strategic options against predefined criteria, and produces documented strategies that address protection, stabilization, continuity, and recovery requirements. This structured approach ensures that selected strategies can effectively minimize impacts of disruptions and support the achievement of recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs).

**Inputs**:
- Business Impact Analysis results
- Risk Assessment findings
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs)
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs)
- Resource availability constraints
- Budget parameters
- Regulatory requirements
- Stakeholder requirements

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategies
- Strategy selection criteria matrix
- Cost-benefit analysis report
- Resource requirement specifications
- Implementation roadmap
- Strategy approval records

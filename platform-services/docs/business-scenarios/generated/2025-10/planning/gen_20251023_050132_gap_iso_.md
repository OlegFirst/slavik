Title: Operational Planning and Control Implementation

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish, implement, and maintain processes needed to meet business continuity requirements and implement actions identified during planning activities. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system enables organizations to operationalize their business continuity planning through structured implementation of controls, resource allocation, and performance criteria establishment.

The system provides a comprehensive framework for translating strategic business continuity objectives into operational reality. It ensures that planned arrangements are effectively implemented, controlled, and monitored, while maintaining documented information as evidence of process execution. This includes managing both planned changes and unintended changes, reviewing consequences and taking action to mitigate adverse effects as necessary.

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains operational planning and control processes to ensure effective implementation of business continuity arrangements. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system enables organizations to plan, implement, and control the processes needed to meet business continuity requirements and implement actions determined necessary to address risks and opportunities.

The framework provides structured mechanisms for defining operational criteria, implementing control measures, and maintaining documented information to demonstrate that processes have been carried out as planned. This ensures consistent application of business continuity procedures across all organizational levels and locations.

**Description**:
Organizations must establish, implement, and maintain processes needed to meet business continuity requirements and implement actions determined during planning activities. This scenario demonstrates how the BCM system enables organizations to operationalize their business continuity planning through structured controls, resource allocation, and performance criteria definition.

The system provides comprehensive operational planning capabilities that ensure business continuity processes are effectively controlled, monitored, and maintained. By establishing clear criteria for processes, implementing necessary changes, and maintaining documented information, organizations can demonstrate their commitment to operational excellence in business continuity management.

Title: Business Continuity Planning and Operational Control Implementation

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the establishment and implementation of operational planning and control processes required for effective business continuity management. Organizations must plan, implement, and control the processes needed to meet BCMS requirements and implement actions determined during risk assessment and business impact analysis.

The scenario covers the systematic approach to developing, documenting, and maintaining business continuity procedures, including resource allocation, competency requirements, and performance criteria. It ensures that planned changes are controlled and unintended changes are reviewed, with actions taken to mitigate adverse effects. This comprehensive operational control framework enables organizations to maintain their business continuity capabilities while adapting to changing business environments and emerging threats.

**Inputs**:
- Risk assessment results and treatment plans
- Business impact analysis findings
- Resource requirements and constraints
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Performance objectives and criteria
- Change requests and proposals
- Lessons learned from exercises and incidents

**Outputs**:
- Documented business continuity procedures
- Resource allocation plans
- Operational control measures
- Change control records
- Performance monitoring reports
- Corrective action plans
- Updated BCMS documentation

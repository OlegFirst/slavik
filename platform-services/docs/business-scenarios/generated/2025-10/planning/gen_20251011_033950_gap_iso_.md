Title: Business Continuity Objectives Setting and Performance Tracking
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish measurable business continuity objectives at relevant functions and levels, ensuring alignment with the business continuity policy and strategic direction. This scenario enables the systematic definition, documentation, and monitoring of BC objectives across different organizational levels, from corporate-wide goals to department-specific targets.

The system facilitates the creation of SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) objectives, linking them to specific risks, critical activities, and recovery requirements identified through BIA and risk assessment processes. Each objective is assigned ownership, performance indicators, and review cycles to ensure continuous monitoring and achievement tracking.

**Inputs**:
- Business continuity policy requirements
- BIA results (RTOs, RPOs, critical activities)
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Strategic business goals
- Resource availability constraints
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Previous performance data

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC objectives hierarchy
- Performance indicator definitions
- Objective achievement dashboards
- Progress tracking reports
- Objective review schedules
- Resource allocation plans
- Compliance evidence documentation

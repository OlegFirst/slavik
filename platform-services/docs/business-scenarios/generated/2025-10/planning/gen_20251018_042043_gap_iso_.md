Title: Business Continuity Objectives Setting and Review

Service: Planning

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains business continuity objectives that align with its strategic direction and business continuity policy. These objectives are set at relevant functions and levels, taking into consideration the organization's risk appetite, legal requirements, and stakeholder expectations. The system enables systematic definition, tracking, and review of measurable BC objectives with clear ownership and timelines.

This scenario demonstrates how the organization implements a structured approach to setting SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) business continuity objectives. The process includes stakeholder consultation, resource allocation planning, and integration with the overall business continuity management system. Regular reviews ensure objectives remain relevant and achievable as business conditions change.

Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of business impact analysis and risk assessment. Organizations must identify and evaluate multiple strategy options for protecting prioritized activities, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery objectives.

The process ensures that selected strategies adequately address identified risks and meet recovery time objectives while remaining practical and achievable within organizational constraints. Strategy selection involves stakeholder consultation, resource evaluation, and formal approval processes to ensure organizational commitment and readiness.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results with prioritized activities and RTOs
- Risk assessment findings and treatment priorities
- Resource availability and constraints
- Regulatory and contractual requirements
- Technology dependencies and alternatives
- Stakeholder requirements and preferences

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC strategy options evaluation
- Selected strategies with justification
- Resource requirements for each strategy
- Implementation roadmap and timelines
- Strategy approval records
- Gap analysis between current and target capabilities

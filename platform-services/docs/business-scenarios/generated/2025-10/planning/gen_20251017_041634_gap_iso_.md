Title: Business Continuity Strategy Development and Selection

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the systematic development and selection of business continuity strategies based on the outputs of business impact analysis and risk assessment. The organization evaluates multiple strategy options for protecting prioritized activities and their supporting resources, considering factors such as feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and alignment with recovery time objectives.

The process ensures that selected strategies address the protection of people, premises, technology, information, supplies, and stakeholders. Each strategy undergoes evaluation against predefined criteria including implementation timeframe, resource requirements, and potential impact on business operations. This structured approach enables the organization to make informed decisions about which continuity strategies best support their critical activities while maintaining compliance with ISO 22301 requirements.

**Inputs**:
- BIA results with prioritized activities and RTOs
- Risk assessment findings and treatment options
- Resource dependency mapping
- Budget constraints and limitations
- Stakeholder requirements and expectations
- Current capability assessment

**Outputs**:
- Approved BC strategy document
- Strategy selection rationale and justification
- Resource allocation plan
- Implementation roadmap
- Strategy effectiveness metrics
- Gap analysis report

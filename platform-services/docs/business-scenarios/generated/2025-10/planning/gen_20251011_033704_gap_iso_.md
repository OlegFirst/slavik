Title: Business Continuity Objectives Setting and Tracking
Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and monitors business continuity objectives aligned with its strategic direction and risk appetite. The BCM system enables systematic definition, documentation, and tracking of measurable objectives that support the organization's commitment to maintaining critical operations during disruptions. These objectives are cascaded across different organizational levels and integrated with performance management processes.

The scenario demonstrates how business continuity objectives are established based on risk assessment outcomes, business impact analysis results, and stakeholder requirements. The system facilitates regular monitoring of objective achievement through automated tracking mechanisms and provides real-time dashboards for management review. This ensures that business continuity objectives remain relevant, achievable, and aligned with changing business needs.

**Inputs**:
- Strategic business goals and priorities
- Risk assessment results
- Business impact analysis data
- Regulatory and contractual requirements
- Historical incident data
- Resource availability constraints
- Stakeholder expectations

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC objectives with SMART criteria
- Objective achievement dashboards
- Performance tracking reports
- Objective review schedules
- Gap analysis between current and target states
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Objective revision proposals

Title: Establish Operational Planning and Control Framework for BCM Activities

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
An organization implements a comprehensive operational planning and control framework to ensure all business continuity management activities are properly planned, executed, and controlled. This scenario demonstrates how the organization establishes criteria for BCM processes, implements control measures, and maintains documented information to meet operational requirements.

The framework encompasses all BCM operational activities including risk assessments, business impact analyses, strategy development, and plan maintenance. By establishing clear operational controls, the organization ensures consistent execution of BCM activities while maintaining compliance with ISO 22301 requirements. This systematic approach enables the organization to effectively manage planned changes and control unintended changes that could impact business continuity capabilities.

- Stakeholder requirements

**Outputs**:

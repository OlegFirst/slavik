Title: Business Continuity Planning and Operational Control Implementation

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario demonstrates the establishment and implementation of operational planning and control processes required for effective business continuity management. Organizations must plan, implement, and control the processes needed to meet BCMS requirements and implement actions determined during risk assessment and business impact analysis.

The scenario covers the systematic approach to developing, documenting, and maintaining business continuity procedures, including resource allocation, competency requirements, and performance criteria. It ensures that planned arrangements are suitable for achieving business continuity objectives and that changes to these arrangements are controlled and communicated effectively throughout the organization.

**Inputs**:
- Business Impact Analysis results
- Risk Assessment outcomes
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- Resource requirements and constraints
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Previous incident reports and lessons learned

**Outputs**:
- Documented business continuity procedures
- Resource allocation matrix
- Performance criteria and metrics
- Change control records
- Communication protocols
- Training requirements documentation
- Operational control checklists

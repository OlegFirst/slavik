Title: Business Continuity Objectives Setting and Review

Service: Planning
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains business continuity objectives that are consistent with the business continuity policy and aligned with strategic direction. These objectives are set at relevant functions and levels within the organization, taking into account the results of business impact analysis and risk assessment. The system enables systematic definition, tracking, and periodic review of measurable BC objectives.

Each objective is defined with specific criteria including what will be accomplished, required resources, responsible parties, completion timelines, and evaluation methods. The platform ensures objectives remain relevant through automated review cycles and performance tracking against established metrics. This systematic approach ensures business continuity objectives drive continuous improvement and maintain alignment with changing business priorities.

**Inputs**:
- Strategic business goals
- Business continuity policy requirements
- BIA results and critical process priorities
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Resource availability constraints
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Previous objective performance data

**Outputs**:
- Documented BC objectives with SMART criteria
- Objective assignment matrix
- Resource allocation plan
- Performance measurement framework
- Objective review schedule
- Progress tracking dashboard
- Compliance alignment report

Title: Define and Track Business Continuity Objectives and Performance Targets

Service: Planning

Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes measurable business continuity objectives aligned with its strategic direction and business continuity policy. These objectives are set at relevant functions and levels, considering critical business activities identified through BIA and risk assessment outcomes. The system enables systematic planning, monitoring, and achievement tracking of these objectives.

Each objective is defined with specific criteria including what will be accomplished, required resources, responsible parties, completion timeframes, and evaluation methods. The platform facilitates regular review and updates of objectives based on changing business requirements, risk landscape, and performance data. This ensures the BCMS remains effective and aligned with organizational goals.

The scenario demonstrates compliance by providing a structured framework for establishing SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) business continuity objectives. It enables organizations to cascade high-level objectives into operational targets, assign accountability, and track progress through integrated dashboards and reporting mechanisms.

**Inputs**:
- Strategic business continuity policy statements
- BIA results and critical activity priorities
- Risk assessment outcomes
- Resource availability constraints
- Regulatory and contractual requirements
- Previous performance data and lessons learned
- Stakeholder expectations and requirements

**Outputs**:
- Documented business continuity objectives register
- Objective achievement plans with timelines
- Resource allocation matrices
- Performance indicators and targets
- Progress tracking dashboards
- Objective review and update records
- Compliance evidence documentation

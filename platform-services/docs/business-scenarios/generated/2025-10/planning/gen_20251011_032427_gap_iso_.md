Title: Management Review of Business Continuity Performance

Service: Planning
Category: Reports

**Description**:
The organization conducts quarterly management reviews to evaluate the performance and effectiveness of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). Senior leadership examines key performance indicators, incident response metrics, and system improvements to ensure the BCMS remains suitable, adequate, and effective for protecting critical business operations.

During these reviews, management assesses the status of actions from previous reviews, changes in external and internal issues relevant to the BCMS, and opportunities for continual improvement. The review process includes evaluation of business continuity performance against established objectives, feedback from stakeholders, and results from exercises and actual incidents.

**Inputs**:
- Previous management review action items
- Business continuity performance metrics
- Audit findings and non-conformities
- Exercise and test results
- Incident response reports
- Resource utilization data
- Stakeholder feedback
- Risk assessment updates
- Changes in legal and regulatory requirements

**Outputs**:
- Management review meeting minutes
- Decisions on BCMS improvements
- Resource allocation approvals
- Updated business continuity objectives
- Action plan with assigned responsibilities
- Performance improvement initiatives
- Policy and strategy updates

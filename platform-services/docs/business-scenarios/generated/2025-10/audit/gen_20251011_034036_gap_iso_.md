**Description**:
The Internal Audit Program Management scenario enables organizations to establish, implement, and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This scenario provides a structured approach to planning, conducting, and reporting internal audits at planned intervals to determine whether the BCMS conforms to the organization's own requirements and ISO 22301 standards.

The system facilitates audit scheduling based on the importance of processes, changes affecting the organization, and results of previous audits. It ensures objective and impartial audit execution by managing auditor assignments, competency verification, and independence requirements. The scenario includes automated workflows for audit planning, execution, findings management, and corrective action tracking.

This comprehensive audit management capability ensures organizations can systematically evaluate their BCMS effectiveness, identify improvement opportunities, and maintain evidence of conformity. The integrated approach supports both scheduled and triggered audits while maintaining complete audit trails for management review and external certification purposes.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule and frequency requirements
- Process criticality and risk assessment results
- Previous audit findings and corrective actions
- Auditor competency profiles and availability
- BCMS scope and documented procedures
- Audit criteria and checklists
- Organizational changes and incidents
- Resource allocation and constraints

**Outputs**:
- Annual internal audit program
- Individual audit plans with scope and criteria
- Auditor assignment notifications
- Audit findings and non-conformity reports
- Corrective action requests (CARs)
- Audit completion certificates
- Trend analysis and performance metrics
- Management review inputs

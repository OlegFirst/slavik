Service: Audit

**Description**:
The Internal Audit Program Management scenario enables organizations to establish, implement, and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their business continuity management system (BCMS). This scenario provides a structured approach to planning, conducting, and reporting internal audits at planned intervals to determine whether the BCMS conforms to the organization's own requirements and ISO 22301 standards.

The system facilitates the creation of annual audit schedules, assignment of qualified auditors, development of audit criteria and checklists, and tracking of audit findings through to closure. It ensures objectivity and impartiality of the audit process by managing auditor independence and competency requirements. The scenario supports evidence-based auditing with documented audit trails, non-conformity tracking, and corrective action management.

This comprehensive audit management capability ensures organizations can systematically verify the effectiveness of their BCMS implementation, identify areas for improvement, and maintain compliance with ISO 22301 requirements. The system provides management with reliable information about BCMS performance and conformity status.

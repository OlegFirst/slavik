Title: Internal Audit Program Implementation and Execution
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
The organization establishes and maintains a comprehensive internal audit program to evaluate the effectiveness of its Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This scenario demonstrates the systematic approach to planning, conducting, and reporting internal audits at planned intervals to verify that the BCMS conforms to ISO 22301 requirements and the organization's own BCMS requirements.

The internal audit process encompasses all BCMS processes, procedures, and controls across different organizational units and locations. Audits are conducted by trained and qualified internal auditors who are independent of the areas being audited, ensuring objectivity and impartiality in the assessment process.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule and plan
- Previous audit reports and corrective actions
- BCMS documentation and records
- ISO 22301 requirements checklist
- Organizational BCMS requirements
- Risk assessment results
- Performance metrics and KPIs

**Outputs**:

- Non-conformity records
- Audit findings summary
- Corrective action requests
- Audit program effectiveness metrics

Service: Audit

**Description**:
The Internal Audit Program Management scenario enables organizations to establish, implement, and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their business continuity management system (BCMS). This scenario provides a structured approach to planning, conducting, and reporting internal audits at planned intervals to verify that the BCMS conforms to the organization's own requirements and ISO 22301 standards.

The system facilitates the creation of annual audit programs, assignment of qualified auditors, scheduling of audits across different processes and locations, and tracking of audit findings through to closure. It ensures objectivity and impartiality by managing auditor independence, maintaining competency records, and providing automated conflict-of-interest checks. The scenario supports evidence-based auditing with standardized checklists, finding categorization, and root cause analysis capabilities.

This comprehensive audit management approach ensures systematic evaluation of BCMS effectiveness, identifies opportunities for improvement, and provides management with reliable information about the system's performance. The automated workflow ensures consistent audit execution while maintaining detailed audit trails for compliance demonstration.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule and scope
- Auditor qualifications and availability
- Previous audit results and corrective actions
- BCMS processes and controls to be audited
- Audit criteria and checklists
- Risk assessment results
- Performance metrics and KPIs

**Outputs**:
- Audit program calendar
- Audit plans and checklists
- Audit reports with findings and recommendations
- Non-conformity reports (NCRs)
- Corrective action requests (CARs)
- Audit performance metrics
- Management review reports

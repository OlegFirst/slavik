Title: Internal Audit Program Management
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario enables organizations to establish and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their business continuity management system (BCMS). The system facilitates planning, conducting, and tracking internal audits to verify conformity with ISO 22301 requirements, organizational policies, and the effectiveness of BCMS implementation.

The platform provides automated audit scheduling based on risk levels, previous audit findings, and organizational changes. It supports the complete audit lifecycle from planning through corrective action verification, ensuring systematic evaluation of all BCMS elements. The system maintains audit independence by tracking auditor qualifications and preventing conflicts of interest in audit assignments.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule requirements
- BCMS scope and boundaries
- Previous audit findings and corrective actions
- Auditor competency profiles
- Risk assessment results
- Process criticality ratings
- Organizational structure and reporting lines

**Outputs**:
- Annual internal audit program
- Individual audit plans
- Audit checklists aligned to ISO 22301
- Audit findings and non-conformity reports
- Corrective action tracking dashboard
- Audit effectiveness metrics
- Management review reports

Title: Internal Audit Program Management
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Internal Audit Program Management scenario enables organizations to establish, implement, and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This scenario provides a structured approach to planning, conducting, and reporting internal audits at planned intervals to determine whether the BCMS conforms to the organization's own requirements and ISO 22301 standards.

The system facilitates the creation of annual audit schedules, assignment of qualified auditors, development of audit criteria and checklists, and tracking of audit findings through to closure. By automating the audit workflow and providing real-time visibility into audit status and findings, organizations can ensure objective and impartial assessment of their BCMS effectiveness while maintaining documented evidence of the audit program's implementation.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule requirements
- Audit scope and criteria definitions
- Auditor qualifications and availability
- Previous audit results and corrective actions
- BCMS documentation and records
- Risk assessment results
- Process performance metrics

**Outputs**:
- Annual internal audit schedule
- Audit plans and checklists
- Audit reports with findings and recommendations
- Non-conformity reports
- Corrective action requests
- Audit program performance metrics
- Management review inputs

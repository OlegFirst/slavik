Title: Internal Audit Program Management
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program to verify that their business continuity management system conforms to ISO 22301 requirements and their own BCM requirements. This scenario enables systematic planning, execution, and tracking of internal audits across all BCM processes, ensuring objective evidence of compliance and continuous improvement opportunities are identified.

The system facilitates audit scheduling based on risk priorities and previous audit findings, manages auditor assignments considering independence requirements, and tracks audit execution from planning through corrective action closure. Automated workflows ensure timely completion of audit activities and escalation of overdue items to appropriate management levels.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule requirements
- Risk assessment results
- Previous audit findings and corrective actions
- Auditor competency profiles
- BCM process scope and boundaries
- Audit criteria and checklists
- Resource availability constraints

**Outputs**:
- Annual internal audit program
- Individual audit plans
- Audit findings and non-conformities
- Audit reports with evidence
- Corrective action requests
- Audit performance metrics
- Compliance status dashboard

Title: Internal Audit Program Management
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
Organizations must establish and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program to verify that their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) conforms to ISO 22301 requirements and is effectively implemented and maintained. This scenario enables the planning, execution, and tracking of internal audits across all BCMS processes, ensuring systematic evaluation of compliance gaps and continuous improvement opportunities.

The system facilitates audit scheduling based on risk levels and previous findings, assigns qualified auditors, manages audit checklists and evidence collection, and tracks corrective actions through to closure. Automated workflows ensure that audit activities are conducted at planned intervals, findings are properly documented, and management receives timely reports on BCMS performance and compliance status.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule and scope
- Auditor qualifications and availability
- Previous audit findings and corrective actions
- BCMS process documentation
- Risk assessment results
- Compliance requirements checklist
- Evidence collection templates

**Outputs**:
- Audit plans and schedules
- Audit notification letters
- Completed audit checklists
- Non-conformity reports
- Audit findings summary
- Corrective action requests
- Management review reports
- Audit completion certificates

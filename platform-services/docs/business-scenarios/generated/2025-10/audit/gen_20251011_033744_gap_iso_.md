Title: Internal Audit Program for Business Continuity Management System

Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario establishes a comprehensive internal audit program to evaluate the effectiveness and compliance of the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) with ISO 22301 requirements. The organization conducts planned audits at defined intervals to verify that the BCMS conforms to organizational requirements and the international standard, while identifying opportunities for improvement.

The internal audit program encompasses all BCMS processes, procedures, and controls across the organization. Audits are scheduled based on the importance of processes, previous audit results, and changes to the organization. The program ensures objective and impartial assessment through qualified internal auditors who are independent of the areas being audited.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule and scope
- Previous audit reports and corrective actions
- BCMS documentation and records
- Process performance metrics
- Risk assessment results
- Compliance requirements checklist
- Audit criteria and methodology

**Outputs**:
- Internal audit reports with findings
- Non-conformity records
- Corrective action requests
- Audit performance metrics
- Compliance status dashboard
- Improvement recommendations
- Audit program effectiveness review

Title: Internal Audit Program Management
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
This scenario enables organizations to establish and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their business continuity management system (BCMS). The system facilitates planning, conducting, and tracking internal audits to verify conformity with ISO 22301 requirements, organizational policies, and the effectiveness of BCMS implementation.

The audit management module provides automated scheduling based on risk levels, previous audit findings, and organizational changes. It ensures systematic coverage of all BCMS processes, maintains audit independence through automated auditor assignment, and tracks corrective actions through completion. The system generates audit reports and performance metrics to demonstrate continuous improvement and compliance with ISO 22301 Clause 9.2 requirements.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule parameters
- BCMS scope and processes to be audited
- Auditor qualifications and availability
- Previous audit findings and corrective actions
- Risk assessment results
- Organizational changes and incidents
- Audit criteria and checklists

**Outputs**:
- Annual internal audit program
- Individual audit plans
- Audit findings and non-conformity reports
- Corrective action requests (CARs)
- Audit completion certificates
- Management review reports
- Audit effectiveness metrics

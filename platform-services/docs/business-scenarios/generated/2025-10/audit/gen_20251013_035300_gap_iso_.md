Title: Internal Audit Program Management
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Internal Audit Program Management scenario enables organizations to establish, implement, and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This scenario facilitates the planning, execution, and tracking of internal audits at planned intervals to determine whether the BCMS conforms to the organization's own requirements and ISO 22301 standards.

The system provides automated audit scheduling based on risk levels, previous audit findings, and organizational changes. It manages the complete audit lifecycle from planning through corrective action verification, ensuring systematic coverage of all BCMS elements. The scenario includes competency tracking for auditors, audit checklist management, and real-time collaboration features for audit teams.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule requirements
- BCMS scope and boundaries
- Previous audit findings and corrective actions
- Auditor competency profiles
- Risk assessment results
- Process criticality ratings
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- Audit criteria and checklists

**Outputs**:
- Annual internal audit program
- Individual audit plans
- Audit findings reports
- Non-conformity records
- Corrective action requests
- Audit effectiveness metrics
- Auditor performance evaluations
- Management review inputs

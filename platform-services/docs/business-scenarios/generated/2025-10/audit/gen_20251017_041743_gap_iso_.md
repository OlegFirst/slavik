Title: Internal Audit Program Management
Service: Audit
Category: Core

**Description**:
The Internal Audit Program Management scenario enables organizations to establish, implement, and maintain a comprehensive internal audit program for their Business Continuity Management System (BCMS). This systematic approach ensures that all BCMS processes, procedures, and controls are regularly evaluated against ISO 22301 requirements and organizational objectives.

The scenario facilitates planning and conducting internal audits at defined intervals, managing audit schedules, assigning qualified auditors, tracking findings, and monitoring corrective actions. It provides a structured framework for determining audit criteria, scope, frequency, and methods while ensuring objectivity and impartiality of the audit process. This comprehensive audit management capability helps organizations identify improvement opportunities, verify compliance, and demonstrate the effectiveness of their BCMS to stakeholders.

**Inputs**:
- Annual audit schedule and frequency requirements
- BCMS scope and process boundaries
- Previous audit findings and corrective actions
- Auditor qualifications and availability
- ISO 22301 requirements checklist
- Organizational BCMS objectives and performance criteria
- Risk assessment results and criticality ratings

**Outputs**:
- Approved internal audit program
- Individual audit plans with defined scope and criteria
- Audit findings and non-conformity reports
- Corrective action requests (CARs)
- Audit performance metrics and trends
- Management review reports
- Auditor competency records

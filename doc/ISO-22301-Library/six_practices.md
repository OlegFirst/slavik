# BCI Good Practice Guidelines (GPG) 7.0
## Six Professional Practices

**Source:** Business Continuity Institute (BCI)
**Edition:** 7.0 (November 2023)
**Purpose:** Industry standard framework for implementing Business Continuity

---

## OVERVIEW

BCI GPG consists of **6 Professional Practices** (PP):
- **2 Management Practices** (PP1, PP2)
- **4 Technical Practices** (PP3, PP4, PP5, PP6)

These practices align with ISO 22301 and provide practical implementation guidance.

---

## PP1: ESTABLISHING A BCMS

**Formerly:** Policy and Programme Management

### Purpose
Establish governance, framework, and foundation for business continuity across the organization.

### Key Activities

1. **Establish BC Policy**
   - Define purpose and scope
   - Align with organizational objectives
   - Obtain top management commitment
   - Communicate to all stakeholders

2. **Define BCMS Scope**
   - Identify what's included/excluded
   - Consider organizational boundaries
   - Document clearly

3. **Develop BC Programme**
   - Create roadmap for implementation
   - Define milestones and deliverables
   - Allocate resources and budget
   - Establish timelines

4. **Establish Governance**
   - Define roles and responsibilities
   - Create BC steering committee
   - Assign BC Manager/Coordinator
   - Define reporting structures

5. **Define Objectives and Metrics**
   - Set measurable BC objectives
   - Define KPIs for monitoring
   - Establish baseline

### ISO 22301 Mapping
- Clause 4 (Context)
- Clause 5 (Leadership)
- Clause 6 (Planning)

### Deliverables
- BC Policy (approved by top management)
- BCMS scope statement
- BC programme plan
- Organizational structure with BC roles
- BC objectives and KPIs

## PP2: EMBRACING BUSINESS CONTINUITY

**Formerly:** Embedding Business Continuity

### Purpose
Foster BC culture across organization, ensure awareness, competence, and engagement at all levels.

### Key Activities

1. **BC Awareness Programme**
   - Communicate BC importance to all staff
   - Explain individual roles in BC
   - Use multiple communication channels
   - Regular awareness campaigns

2. **Training and Education**
   - Identify competency requirements for BC roles
   - Develop training curriculum
   - Deliver role-specific training:
     - BC team members (advanced)
     - Line managers (intermediate)
     - All staff (basic awareness)
   - Track training completion

3. **Culture Building**
   - Integrate BC into organizational values
   - Recognize BC champions
   - Encourage BC mindset in daily operations
   - Make BC "business as usual"

4. **Engagement**
   - Involve leadership visibly
   - Engage employees at all levels
   - Foster ownership of BC responsibilities
   - Create feedback mechanisms

### ISO 22301 Mapping
- Clause 5.3 (Roles and responsibilities)
- Clause 7.2 (Competence)
- Clause 7.3 (Awareness)
- Clause 7.4 (Communication)

### Deliverables
- BC awareness materials
- Training curriculum and materials
- Competency matrix
- Training records
- Communication plans
- Culture assessment

### Healthcare Considerations
- Patient safety as BC driver
- Clinical staff BC training
- Regulatory awareness (HIPAA, etc.)
- Multi-disciplinary engagement

## PP3: ANALYSIS

### Purpose
Understand the organization's critical activities, impacts of disruption, and risks to enable informed BC decisions.

### Key Activities

#### A. Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

**1. Identify Critical Activities**
   - Map business processes
   - Identify products and services
   - Understand value chains
   - Determine criticality

**2. Assess Impacts Over Time**
   - Financial impact (revenue loss, costs)
   - Operational impact (productivity, capacity)
   - Reputational impact (brand, customer confidence)
   - Regulatory impact (compliance, penalties)
   - Safety impact (people, environment)

**3. Determine Time Frames**
   - **RTO (Recovery Time Objective):** Max acceptable downtime
   - **RPO (Recovery Point Objective):** Max acceptable data loss
   - **MTPD (Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption):** Absolute limit
   - Prioritization based on impact thresholds

**4. Identify Dependencies**
   - People (key personnel, skills)
   - Technology (systems, applications, data)
   - Facilities (buildings, workspace)
   - Suppliers (vendors, partners)
   - Infrastructure (utilities, communications)

**5. Resource Requirements**
   - Minimum resources to maintain critical activities
   - Peak demand resources
   - Recovery resources

#### B. Risk Assessment

**1. Identify Threats and Hazards**
   - Natural (earthquake, flood, pandemic)
   - Technological (IT failure, cyber attack)
   - Human (error, sabotage, terrorism)
   - Business (supplier failure, market collapse)

**2. Analyze Risk**
   - **Likelihood:** Probability of occurrence
   - **Impact:** Severity of consequences
   - Risk level = Likelihood × Impact

**3. Evaluate and Prioritize Risks**
   - Determine which risks need treatment
   - Consider risk appetite/tolerance
   - Prioritize based on risk level

**4. Identify Risk Concentrations**
   - Single points of failure
   - Common dependencies
   - Geographic concentrations
   - Cascading risks

### ISO 22301 Mapping
- Clause 6.1 (Risks and opportunities)
- Clause 8.2 (BIA and risk assessment)

### Deliverables
- BIA methodology
- BIA reports for all critical processes
- Impact assessment (qualitative and quantitative)
- RTO/RPO/MTPD definitions
- Dependency mapping
- Risk assessment methodology
- Risk register
- Risk treatment priorities

### Healthcare Specific BIA

**Critical Services Tiers:**
- **Tier 1 (Immediate):** Emergency care, ICU (RTO: 0)
- **Tier 2 (Urgent):** Surgery, lab, pharmacy (RTO: 2-4 hours)
- **Tier 3 (Important):** Outpatient, admin (RTO: 1 day)
- **Tier 4 (Normal):** Non-critical (RTO: 3-5 days)

**Impact Categories:**
- Patient safety and clinical outcomes
- Regulatory compliance (HIPAA, CMS)
- Reputational damage to hospital/NPO
- Financial (reimbursement loss)
- Legal liability

## PP4: SOLUTIONS DESIGN

**Formerly:** Design

### Purpose
Develop appropriate strategies and solutions to address impacts and risks identified in analysis.

### Key Activities

1. **Strategy Options Development**

   **Pre-Incident Strategies (Mitigation):**
   - Redundancy (backup systems, duplicate resources)
   - Diversification (multiple suppliers)
   - Resilience building (robust infrastructure)
   - Prevention measures (security, maintenance)

   **During-Incident Strategies (Response):**
   - Incident management structure
   - Emergency response procedures
   - Damage limitation
   - Stakeholder communication

   **Post-Incident Strategies (Recovery):**
   - Work area recovery (alternate sites)
   - Technology recovery (IT DR)
   - People strategies (remote work, cross-training)
   - Supply chain alternatives

2. **Strategy Selection**
   - Evaluate options against:
     - Cost vs. benefit
     - Feasibility
     - Alignment with RTO/RPO
     - Risk reduction
   - Select optimal strategies
   - Document rationale

3. **Resource Identification**
   - Identify resources needed for each strategy:
     - Recovery sites (hot/warm/cold)
     - Technology (backup systems, communications)
     - People (recovery teams, specialists)
     - Supplies (emergency stocks)
     - Financial (insurance, emergency funds)

4. **Recovery Prioritization**
   - Sequence recovery activities
   - Align with BIA priorities
   - Consider dependencies

### ISO 22301 Mapping
- Clause 8.3 (Business continuity strategy)

### Deliverables
- BC strategy document
- Strategy options analysis
- Selected strategies with justification
- Resource requirements
- Recovery timeline
- Cost-benefit analysis

### Healthcare Strategies

**Work Area Recovery:**
- Alternate care sites
- Mobile medical units
- Telemedicine capabilities
- Mutual aid agreements with other hospitals

**Technology Recovery:**
- EHR (Electronic Health Records) backup
- PACS (Picture Archiving) redundancy
- Lab systems DR
- Pharmacy systems failover

**Supply Chain:**
- Critical medical supplies stockpile
- Pharmaceutical backup suppliers
- PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) reserves
- Oxygen/medical gas backup

## PP5: ENABLING SOLUTIONS

**Formerly:** Implementation

### Purpose
Implement BC strategies through documented plans, procedures, and response capabilities.

### Key Activities

1. **Develop BC Plans**

   **Types of Plans:**
   - Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
   - Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
   - Incident Response Plan (IRP)
   - Crisis Management Plan (CMP)
   - Crisis Communication Plan

   **Plan Structure:**
   - Purpose and scope
   - Activation criteria and authority
   - Roles and responsibilities
   - Notification and escalation
   - Response procedures (step-by-step)
   - Recovery procedures
   - Resource lists
   - Contact information
   - Forms and checklists

2. **Establish Response Organization**
   - Incident Management Team (IMT)
   - Crisis Management Team (CMT)
   - Recovery teams (by function/process)
   - Define roles (commander, coordinator, specialist)
   - Create call trees and rosters
   - 24/7 contact availability

3. **Develop Procedures**
   - Detailed step-by-step procedures
   - Job aids and checklists
   - Decision trees
   - Escalation matrices
   - Playbooks for scenarios

4. **Communication Capabilities**
   - Emergency notification systems
   - Multiple communication channels
   - Stakeholder messaging templates
   - Media relations protocols
   - Internal communications

5. **Resource Arrangements**
   - Contracts with alternate site providers
   - Technology recovery arrangements
   - Supplier agreements
   - Mutual aid agreements
   - Insurance coverage

### ISO 22301 Mapping
- Clause 8.4 (BC plans and procedures)
- Clause 8.4.2 (Incident response structure)
- Clause 8.4.3 (Warning and communication)

### Deliverables
- BC plans for all critical processes
- Incident response procedures
- Recovery procedures
- Contact lists and call trees
- Communication templates
- Response team structure
- Resource contracts/agreements
- Plan distribution and access

### Healthcare Plans

**Clinical Continuity Plans:**
- Emergency department surge plan
- ICU capacity expansion
- Operating room continuity
- Lab services continuity
- Pharmacy services continuity
- Patient evacuation plan

**Support Service Plans:**
- IT/EHR disaster recovery
- Facilities management
- Supply chain continuity
- Staff support (housing, childcare during crisis)

## PP6: VALIDATION

### Purpose
Verify that BC arrangements work as intended through exercising, testing, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

### Key Activities

1. **Exercising and Testing**

   **Exercise Types (Progressive Complexity):**

   - **Desktop Exercise (Tabletop)**
     - Discussion-based
     - Talk through scenario
     - Test decision-making and coordination
     - Low resource, high value

   - **Walkthrough**
     - Step through procedures
     - Verify understanding
     - Identify gaps

   - **Simulation**
     - Simulate incident conditions
     - Test response without full activation
     - Use scenario inject

   - **Full-Scale Exercise**
     - Actual activation of plans
     - Real resource deployment
     - Time-pressured
     - Multi-team coordination

   - **Component Testing**
     - Test specific elements (e.g., IT failover)
     - Technical validation
     - Verify RTOs achieved

   **Exercise Programme:**
   - Annual schedule
   - Mix of exercise types
   - Test all critical processes over cycle
   - Vary scenarios
   - Include expected and unexpected events

2. **Performance Monitoring**

   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
   - % critical processes with BC plans
   - Plan currency (last update date)
   - Exercise frequency and participation
   - RTO achievement in tests
   - Training completion rates
   - Audit findings (open/closed)
   - Incident response times

   **Metrics Collection:**
   - Establish measurement methods
   - Collect data regularly
   - Analyze trends
   - Report to management

3. **Audit and Review**

   - **Internal Audits:**
     - Scheduled audits (annual minimum)
     - Clause-by-clause compliance check
     - Evidence verification
     - Interview stakeholders
     - Report findings

   - **Management Reviews:**
     - Quarterly or bi-annual
     - Top management participation
     - Review metrics, audit results, exercise outcomes
     - Assess BCMS effectiveness
     - Make strategic decisions

   - **Plan Reviews:**
     - Review plans at least annually
     - After exercises
     - After actual incidents
     - After organizational changes

4. **Continuous Improvement**

   - **Lessons Learned:**
     - Capture from exercises
     - Capture from real incidents
     - Capture from audits
     - Analyze root causes

   - **Corrective Actions:**
     - Address nonconformities
     - Implement improvements
     - Track to closure
     - Verify effectiveness

   - **Maturity Assessment:**
     - Assess BC maturity level
     - Benchmark against industry
     - Identify advancement opportunities

### ISO 22301 Mapping
- Clause 8.5 (Exercising and testing)
- Clause 9.1 (Monitoring and measurement)
- Clause 9.2 (Internal audit)
- Clause 9.3 (Management review)
- Clause 10 (Improvement)

### Deliverables
- Exercise schedule
- Exercise plans and scenarios
- Exercise reports with lessons learned
- Corrective action register
- KPI dashboard
- Audit programme
- Audit reports
- Management review minutes
- Improvement action plans
- Maturity assessment

### Healthcare Validation

**Healthcare-Specific Exercises:**
- Mass casualty incident
- Pandemic response
- Ransomware/cyber attack on EHR
- Evacuation (fire, flood)
- Utility failure (power, water, HVAC)
- Supply chain disruption (drug shortage)

**Regulatory Requirements:**
- CMS requires annual emergency preparedness exercises
- Joint Commission standards for drills
- State/local requirements

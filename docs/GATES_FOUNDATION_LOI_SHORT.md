## Who We Are

AI-Platform-ISO is the world's first AI-powered Business Continuity Management (BCM) platform designed for healthcare organizations in LMICs. We replace $150K consultants with 26 AI specialists, delivering ISO 22301-compliant BCM at $10K per organization (93% cost reduction).

**Platform proof:**
- 356,679 lines of code built in 6 months (human-AI partnership: 20x productivity gain)
- 40+ microservices, 81% ISO 22301 compliant, 79/100 security score
- 26 AI specialists (BIA, Risk, Compliance, Planning) + 347+ healthcare case library
- Production-ready with 30-day remediation (security/governance gaps)

BCM Philosophy: Two Paths in Crisis

Crises are inevitable. The question is not IF, but WHEN. Organizations face a critical choice.

WITHOUT BCM: Fragile

  • No clear plan when crisis hits
  • Confusion and delays
  • Improvisation under pressure
  • Services disrupted
  • Patients unable to access care
  • Staff overwhelmed
  • Community loses trust
  • Lives at risk
  • Long, costly recovery

WITH BCM / ISO 22301: Resilient

  • Plan activated immediately
  • Trained response team
  • Clear procedures
  • Services maintained
  • Critical care continues
  • Patients receive treatment
  • Staff protected and supported
  • Community reassured
  • Rapid recovery and learning
  • Organization becomes stronger

Core Philosophy

Preparedness is not optional. It's our professional responsibility to the communities we serve. BCM provides systematic preparation. ISO 22301 provides the proven international framework. Recovery costs for unprepared organizations far exceed preparation investment.

The Continuous Resilience Cycle

BCM is not a one-time project - it's a living system that strengthens your organization with every cycle

1. PREPARE

  • Identify critical services
  • Assess risks
  • Build response plans
  • Train staff
  • Test procedures

2. READY STATE

  • Continuous monitoring
  • Early warning systems
  • Plans accessible
  • Team ready
  • Threat detection

3. RESPOND

  • Activate plan immediately
  • Deploy response teams
  • Protect critical services
  • Support staff
  • Communicate clearly

4. RECOVER

  • Restore services
  • Return to normal
  • Support staff recovery
  • Stabilize operations
  • Document learnings

5. LEARN

What worked well? What needs improvement? Update plans based on real experience. Share knowledge across the organization. Each crisis makes the system stronger.

Then PREPARE again - but now STRONGER with new knowledge

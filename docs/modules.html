Key Service Dependencies

How services work together to deliver comprehensive BCM

BIA → Risk → Strategy Flow

  1. BIA Service identifies critical processes and RTO/RPO
  2. Risk Service assesses threats to those processes
  3. Planning Service develops strategies based on BIA + Risk
  4. Plans Service creates detailed recovery procedures
  5. Validation Service tests the plans through exercises

Incident Response Flow

  1. Event Intelligence detects anomaly/incident
  2. Response Service activates incident workflow
  3. Plans Service retrieves relevant recovery plans
  4. Documents Service provides procedure documents
  5. Learning Service captures lessons learned

Compliance Monitoring Flow

  1. Compliance Service tracks ISO implementation
  2. Governance Service ensures policy adherence
  3. Documents Service manages compliance evidence
  4. Validation Service validates through audits
  5. Learning Service trains staff on requirements

AI-Powered Automation Flow

  1. Expertise Center provides domain specialists
  2. AI Foundation handles LLM orchestration
  3. Workflow Intelligence manages process flows
  4. Predictive Analytics forecasts outcomes
  5. Services consume AI recommendations

Business Continuity Management Philosophy

Two Paths in Crisis: Fragile vs. Resilient

Healthcare organizations face inevitable disruptions: epidemics, natural disasters, cyber attacks, supply chain failures. Yet 92% of healthcare organizations lack comprehensive BCM plans.

✗ WITHOUT BCM: Fragile

  • No clear plan when crisis hits
  • Confusion and delays
  • Improvisation under pressure
  • Services disrupted
  • Patients unable to access care
  • Staff overwhelmed
  • Community loses trust
  • Lives at risk
  • Recovery: 18-24 months
  • Costs: 3-5× higher than preparation

✓ WITH BCM / ISO 22301: Resilient

  • Plan activated immediately
  • Trained response teams

  • Services maintained

  • Patients receive treatment
  • Staff protected and supported
  • Community reassured
  • Rapid recovery and learning
  • Organization becomes stronger

Core Principle

Crises are inevitable. The question is not IF, but WHEN.

Preparedness is not optional — it's our professional responsibility to the communities we serve. BCM provides systematic preparation. ISO 22301 provides the proven international framework.

What is Business Continuity Management?

BCM answers three critical questions

1. Which functions are critical?

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

  • Critical: Emergency care (RTO: 0 min)
  • Critical: Chronic patient treatment (RTO: 4 hours)
  • Critical: Vaccination cold chain (RTO: 2 hours)
  • Important: Scheduled appointments (RTO: 24 hours)
  • Important: Administrative functions (RTO: 3 days)

2. What can go wrong?

Risk Assessment

  • Epidemic: Infectious disease outbreak, staff infection
  • Infrastructure: Power outage, water loss, heating
  • Cyber: Ransomware, data breach
  • Human: Mass illness, key personnel unavailable
  • Logistics: Supply disruption, transport problems

3. How will we continue?

Business Continuity Plans (BCP)

  • Immediate (0-2 hours): Activate protocols, protect staff
  • Short-term (2-24 hours): Mobilize resources
  • Medium-term (1-7 days): Maintain operations
  • Recovery: Document lessons, update plans

# Business Continuity Policy

## 4. Context of the organization

Our organization has identified key stakeholders and requirements for business continuity management.

## 5. Leadership

Management is committed to implementing and maintaining an effective BCMS.

## 6. Planning

### 6.1 Risk Assessment
Regular risk assessments are conducted using FAIR methodology.

### 6.2 Business Impact Analysis
BIA is performed annually to identify critical processes.

## 7. Support

### 7.1 Resources
Adequate resources are allocated for BCM activities.

### 7.2 Competence
Staff receive regular training on business continuity procedures.

## 8. Operation

### 8.1 Incident Response
Incident response procedures are documented and tested quarterly.

### 8.2 Crisis Management
Crisis management team is activated for major incidents.

## 9. Performance evaluation

### 9.1 Monitoring
Key metrics are monitored continuously.

### 9.2 Internal audit
Internal audits conducted semi-annually.

## 10. Improvement

Continuous improvement through PDCA cycle.

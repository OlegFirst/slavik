## OVERVIEW OF THE THREE GUIDES

### 1. BSI ISO 22301 Implementation Guide (10 MB)
- **Publisher**: British Standards Institution
- **Focus**: Practical implementation journey with client testimonials
- **Approach**: Clause-by-clause implementation with training academy support
- **Unique Features**: Business improvement software integration, certification journey roadmap

### 2. NQA ISO 22301:2019 Implementation Guide (3.5 MB)
- **Publisher**: NQA Certification Body
- **Focus**: Certification-focused implementation with detailed clause requirements
- **Approach**: Step-by-step compliance approach with 10-step certification process
- **Unique Features**: Associate Partner Programme, audit-focused guidance

### 3. ISO 22301:2019 Official Implementation Guide (922 KB)
- **Publisher**: ISO Official
- **Focus**: Concise standard interpretation and application
- **Approach**: Core requirements and documentation framework
- **Unique Features**: Minimal prescriptive requirements, flexibility emphasis

## COMMON IMPLEMENTATION FLOWS ACROSS ALL 3 GUIDES

### Core Implementation Cycle (PDCA-Based)

All three guides emphasize the Plan-Do-Check-Act continuous improvement cycle:

```
PLAN → DO → CHECK → ACT
  ↑                      ↓
  ←──────────────────────
```

#### 1. PLAN Phase
- Understand organizational context (internal & external)
- Identify interested parties and their requirements
- Define BCMS scope
- Conduct Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Perform Risk Assessment
- Set business continuity objectives
- Develop BC strategies and solutions

#### 2. DO Phase
- Implement BC plans and procedures
- Establish response structures
- Allocate resources (people, technology, facilities)
- Conduct awareness training
- Document procedures and plans
- Establish communication protocols

#### 3. CHECK Phase
- Monitor and measure BC arrangements
- Conduct BC exercises and tests
- Perform internal audits
- Evaluate BC documentation effectiveness
- Management review

#### 4. ACT Phase
- Address non-conformities
- Implement corrective actions
- Update BC plans based on lessons learned
- Continual improvement initiatives

## PHASED IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAPS

### BSI Implementation Journey (4-Phase Approach)

#### Phase 1: Understand and Prepare (1-2 months)
**Objectives**: Build foundation and secure commitment

**Activities**:
1. Buy the ISO 22301 standard
2. Discover information via case studies and whitepapers
3. Attend BSI ISO 22301 Requirements training course
4. Download self-assessment checklist
5. Secure top management commitment
6. Define scope of BCMS
7. Identify internal/external issues
8. List interested parties

**Deliverables**:
- Executive support letter
- BCMS scope document
- Initial context analysis
- Stakeholder map

**Success Criteria**:
- Top management signed commitment
- Budget allocated
- Project team assigned

---

#### Phase 2: See How Ready You Are (1-2 months)
**Objectives**: Assess current state and plan implementation

**Activities**:
1. Attend BSI Implementing ISO 22301 training course
2. Book BSI gap assessment
3. Review existing BC arrangements
4. Map current processes to ISO 22301 clauses
5. Identify documentation gaps
6. Create implementation plan

**Deliverables**:
- Gap assessment report
- Implementation roadmap
- Resource requirements
- Training needs analysis

**Success Criteria**:
- Clear understanding of gaps
- Realistic timeline established
- Resources committed

---

#### Phase 3: Review and Get Certified (3-6 months)
**Objectives**: Build and implement BCMS

**Activities**:
1. Develop BC Policy
2. Conduct Business Impact Analysis
3. Perform Risk Assessments
4. Develop BC strategies
5. Write BC plans and procedures
6. Implement documented information controls
7. Conduct awareness training
8. Perform BC exercises
9. Conduct internal audits
10. Hold management review
11. Book BSI certification assessment (Stage 1 & Stage 2)

**Deliverables**:
- Complete BCMS documentation
- Tested BC plans
- Internal audit reports
- Management review minutes
- Evidence of 3-6 months operation

**Success Criteria**:
- All documentation in place
- Evidence of implementation
- Internal audit completed
- Management review held

---

#### Phase 4: Continually Improve (Ongoing)
**Objectives**: Maintain and enhance BCMS

**Activities**:
1. Celebrate certification achievement
2. Use BSI Assurance Mark on marketing materials
3. Conduct regular BC exercises
4. Perform surveillance audits
5. Review and update BC arrangements
6. Consider integration with other management systems
7. Engage BSI Client Manager for support
8. Book additional training courses

**Deliverables**:
- Continual improvement register
- Updated BC plans
- Surveillance audit reports
- Performance metrics dashboard

**Success Criteria**:
- Certification maintained
- Demonstrated improvement
- Positive audit results

## BSI-SPECIFIC PRACTICAL FLOWS

### 1. Top Management Engagement Flow

```
Initial Approach → Business Case Development → Executive Presentation →
Budget Approval → Champion Assignment → Kick-off Meeting
```

**Key Activities**:
- **Week 1**: Research business continuity requirements for your industry
- **Week 2**: Develop cost-benefit analysis showing ROI of certification
- **Week 3**: Create executive presentation (15-20 slides maximum)
- **Week 4**: Present to board/executive team
- **Week 5**: Secure budget and resource allocation
- **Week 6**: Assign BC Champion and project team

**BSI Tips**:
> "The earlier that organizations talk to senior managers, the better it will go for them so have those discussions early" - John Scott, Overbury

**Success Factors**:
- Focus on business benefits, not just compliance
- Quantify potential losses from disruptions
- Show competitive advantage
- Demonstrate stakeholder expectations

### 2. Document Development Flow

```
Identify Requirements → Select Templates → Customize for Organization →
Review with Stakeholders → Approve → Implement → Train → Audit
```

**Document Hierarchy** (BSI Approach):
1. **Tier 1**: BC Policy (1-2 pages)
2. **Tier 2**: BCMS Manual (20-30 pages)
3. **Tier 3**: System Procedures (5-10 pages each)
4. **Tier 4**: Work Instructions (1-3 pages each)
5. **Tier 5**: Forms and Templates

**Key Documents with Timeline**:

| Document Type | Development Time | Review Cycles | Approval Level |
|--------------|------------------|---------------|----------------|
| BC Policy | 1 week | 2-3 | Top Management |
| BCMS Manual | 2-3 weeks | 3-4 | BC Champion |
| BIA Procedure | 1 week | 2 | BC Manager |
| BC Plans | 2-4 weeks | 4-6 | Dept. Heads |
| Exercise Procedures | 1 week | 2 | BC Manager |

**BSI Best Practice**:
- Keep documents concise and user-friendly
- Use consistent formatting and terminology
- Include flowcharts and visual aids
- Version control from day one
- Make easily accessible (digital preferred)

### 3. Business Impact Analysis (BIA) Flow

**BSI Detailed BIA Process** (6-8 Weeks):

#### Week 1-2: Preparation
- Identify critical activities
- Select BIA methodology
- Prepare BIA questionnaires
- Identify interviewees
- Schedule BIA sessions

#### Week 3-4: Data Collection
- Conduct interviews with process owners
- Document activity dependencies
- Identify resource requirements
- Determine impact over time
- Establish Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD)

**BIA Interview Template** (BSI):
1. Activity description
2. Products/services delivered
3. Personnel required (number and roles)
4. Technology dependencies
5. Information requirements
6. Facilities needed
7. Suppliers/third parties involved
8. Peak periods/seasonal variations
9. Financial impact (per hour/day/week)
10. Regulatory/legal impacts
11. Reputational consequences
12. Customer impacts

#### Week 5-6: Analysis
- Assess impact across time periods (1hr, 4hr, 8hr, 24hr, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks)
- Calculate financial impacts
- Evaluate operational impacts
- Determine reputational risks
- Prioritize activities by criticality

**BSI Impact Categories**:
- Financial (revenue loss, penalties, additional costs)
- Operational (productivity, service delivery)
- Legal/regulatory (compliance breaches, sanctions)
- Reputation (customer confidence, brand damage)
- Health & Safety (employee/public welfare)

#### Week 7: Reporting
- Create BIA summary report
- Develop criticality matrix
- Present findings to management
- Validate results with stakeholders

#### Week 8: Objective Setting
- Define Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- Set Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- Establish Minimum Business Continuity Objectives (MBCO)
- Document prioritized activities

**BSI Visualization**:
```
Impact Level →
        ↑
        |  CRITICAL (Resume within hours)
        |  ────────────────────────
        |  HIGH (Resume within 1-2 days)
        |  ────────────────────────
        |  MEDIUM (Resume within 1 week)
        |  ────────────────────────
        |  LOW (Resume when convenient)
        └────────────────────────→
                Time →
```

### 4. BC Strategy Development Flow

**BSI 4-Phase Strategy Development**:

#### Phase 1: Option Identification (Week 1-2)
Brainstorm recovery strategies for each critical activity:

**People Strategies**:
- Cross-training and multi-skilling
- Home/remote working
- Outsourcing arrangements
- Reciprocal agreements
- Temporary staff agencies

**Technology Strategies**:
- Redundant systems
- Cloud-based solutions
- Data backup and recovery
- Alternative connectivity
- Manual workarounds

**Facilities Strategies**:
- Alternative work sites
- Hot/warm/cold sites
- Mobile recovery units
- Third-party recovery facilities
- Work-from-home provisions

**Supplier Strategies**:
- Dual sourcing
- Strategic inventory
- Alternative suppliers
- Supplier collaboration agreements

#### Phase 2: Feasibility Assessment (Week 3-4)
Evaluate each option against:
- Cost (capital and operational)
- Time to implement
- Recovery speed
- Reliability
- Scalability
- Complexity

**BSI Decision Matrix**:
| Strategy | Cost | Time to Implement | RTO Achievement | Risk | Score |
|----------|------|-------------------|-----------------|------|-------|
| Option A | High | Long | Fast | Low | 7/10 |
| Option B | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | 6/10 |
| Option C | Low | Short | Slow | High | 4/10 |

#### Phase 3: Solution Selection (Week 5)
- Present options to management
- Consider risk appetite
- Balance cost vs. recovery speed
- Gain approval for preferred solutions

#### Phase 4: Implementation Planning (Week 6-8)
- Create implementation roadmap
- Allocate resources
- Set milestones
- Assign responsibilities
- Begin procurement

### 5. BC Plan Development Flow

#### Plan Components (6-8 Weeks Development):

**1. Plan Administration Section**
- Plan purpose and scope
- Plan ownership and maintenance
- Version control
- Distribution list
- Review schedule

**2. Activation and Notification Section**
- Activation criteria and thresholds
- Decision-making authority
- Notification procedures
- Contact lists (internal/external)
- Escalation process

**3. Response Section**
- Immediate actions (first 4 hours)
- Roles and responsibilities
- Response team structure
- Command center setup
- Safety and welfare procedures

**4. Continuity Section**
- Recovery strategies per activity
- Resource requirements
- Step-by-step procedures
- Workaround processes
- Alternative arrangements

**5. Recovery Section**
- Recovery priorities
- Restoration procedures
- Return to normal operations
- Salvage operations
- Stand-down criteria

**6. Supporting Information**
- Critical documentation
- System information
- Supplier contacts
- Floor plans/site maps
- Insurance details

**BSI Writing Timeline**:
- Week 1-2: Draft plan structure and administration sections
- Week 3-4: Develop response procedures with teams
- Week 5-6: Document continuity arrangements
- Week 7: Compile recovery procedures
- Week 8: Add supporting information and review

**Audience**: Response team members, process owners

**Content**:

### 7. BC Exercise Programme Flow

**BSI Exercise Progression** (Annual Cycle):

#### Q1: Desktop Exercises
**Objective**: Test plan knowledge and decision-making
**Duration**: 2-4 hours
**Participants**: Response team members

**Activities**:
- Scenario presentation
- Discussion-based walk-through
- Decision point analysis
- Role clarification
- Gap identification

**BSI Scenario Examples**:
- IT system failure
- Key supplier disruption
- Facility unavailability
- Staff shortage
- Pandemic response

#### Q2: Functional Exercises
**Objective**: Test specific elements of BC plans
**Duration**: 4-8 hours
**Participants**: Specific teams (IT, facilities, etc.)

**Activities**:
- Activate specific procedures
- Test communication systems
- Verify resource availability
- Practice data recovery
- Evaluate supplier arrangements

#### Q3: Full-Scale Exercises
**Objective**: Test complete BC response
**Duration**: 1-2 days
**Participants**: All response teams

**Activities**:
- Simulate major disruption
- Activate full BC arrangements
- Relocate to alternate site
- Test all systems
- Full stakeholder communication

#### Q4: Review and Planning
**Objective**: Learn and improve
**Duration**: Ongoing

**Activities**:
- Analyze exercise results
- Update plans based on findings
- Schedule next year's exercises
- Address identified gaps
- Report to management

**BSI Exercise Report Template**:
1. Executive summary
2. Exercise objectives
3. Scenario description
4. Participants
5. Observations (strengths/weaknesses)
6. Recommendations
7. Action plan
8. Lessons learned

## NQA CERTIFICATION-FOCUSED FLOWS

### 1. Gap Assessment Flow

**NQA Structured Assessment Process** (2-3 Days):

#### Pre-Assessment Preparation
- Questionnaire completion
- Document review
- Site familiarization
- Stakeholder identification

#### On-Site Assessment Schedule

**Day 1: Documentation Review**
- BCMS manual review
- Policy alignment check
- Procedure adequacy assessment
- Forms and templates review
- Legal requirements verification

**Day 2: Implementation Verification**
- Process observation
- Staff interviews
- Records sampling
- System effectiveness evaluation
- Integration assessment

**Day 3: Reporting and Planning**
- Findings compilation
- Gap prioritization
- Recommendation development
- Implementation timeline
- Resource requirements

#### NQA Gap Analysis Matrix

| Clause | Requirement | Current State | Gap | Priority | Effort |
|--------|-------------|---------------|-----|----------|--------|
| 4.1 | Understand organization context | Partial | Medium | High | 2 weeks |
| 6.1 | Risk assessment process | Not present | Critical | High | 4 weeks |
| 8.4 | BC exercises | Informal | High | Medium | 6 weeks |

**Gap Severity Levels**:
- **Critical**: Missing mandatory requirement, prevents certification
- **Major**: Significant deficiency, must address before certification
- **Minor**: Improvement opportunity, can address after certification
- **Observation**: Suggestion, no formal action required

### 2. Internal Audit Programme Flow

**NQA Annual Audit Cycle**:

#### Planning Phase (Month 1)
**Activities**:
- Develop annual audit schedule
- Allocate auditors to areas
- Define audit scope per area
- Prepare audit checklists
- Communicate schedule

**NQA Risk-Based Scheduling**:
- High-risk processes: Quarterly
- Medium-risk processes: Semi-annually
- Low-risk processes: Annually
- New processes: Within 3 months

#### Preparation Phase (2 Weeks Before)
**Activities**:
- Review previous audit reports
- Check for outstanding actions
- Prepare specific audit questions
- Notify auditees
- Gather relevant documents

#### Execution Phase (1-3 Days)
**NQA Audit Structure**:

**Opening Meeting** (30 minutes):
- Introductions
- Confirm scope and schedule
- Explain audit process
- Set expectations

**Evidence Gathering** (60-80% of time):
- Process observation
- Document review
- Record sampling
- Staff interviews
- System testing

**Findings Analysis** (15% of time):
- Categorize observations
- Identify non-conformities
- Verify positive findings
- Prepare examples

**Closing Meeting** (45 minutes):
- Present findings
- Discuss non-conformities
- Acknowledge good practices
- Agree on timescales
- Thank participants

#### Reporting Phase (1 Week)
**NQA Report Sections**:
1. Audit overview (scope, date, auditors)
2. Executive summary
3. Areas audited
4. Conformities and strengths
5. Non-conformities (categorized)
6. Opportunities for improvement
7. Previous audit follow-up
8. Conclusion and recommendations

#### Follow-Up Phase (Ongoing)
**Activities**:
- Track corrective actions
- Verify effectiveness
- Update audit register
- Report to management
- Plan next cycle

### 5. Supplier BC Evaluation Flow

**NQA Third-Party Assurance Process**:

#### Stage 1: Supplier Identification (Week 1)
**Activities**:
- List all suppliers
- Identify critical suppliers (based on BIA)
- Categorize by criticality
- Prioritize for evaluation

**Criticality Criteria**:
- Single source (no alternatives)
- Long lead time for replacement
- Critical to priority activities
- High impact if disrupted
- Regulatory requirement

#### Stage 2: Evaluation Methodology (Week 2)
**NQA Supplier Assessment Options**:

**Option 1: Questionnaire** (For lower-risk suppliers)
- Self-assessment questionnaire
- BC arrangement description
- Evidence submission
- Desk-based review

**Option 2: On-Site Assessment** (For critical suppliers)
- Site visit
- BC plan review
- Interview with BC coordinator
- Observation of arrangements
- Evidence verification

**Option 3: Certification Verification** (For certified suppliers)
- Request certificate copy
- Verify scope covers your needs
- Check certification body accreditation
- Confirm certificate validity

#### Stage 3: Evaluation Execution (Week 3-8)
**NQA Supplier BC Questionnaire Sections**:

1. **BC Governance**:
   - Do you have a BC policy?
   - Who is responsible for BC?
   - Is BC integrated into operations?

2. **BC Analysis**:
   - Have you conducted a BIA?
   - What are your critical processes?
   - What are your RTOs?

3. **BC Strategies**:
   - What BC solutions are in place?
   - Are there alternative suppliers/sites?
   - How do you maintain capacity?

4. **BC Plans**:
   - Do you have documented BC plans?
   - How often are they tested?
   - When were they last updated?

5. **BC Exercises**:
   - What exercises have you conducted?
   - What were the results?
   - Were actions taken?

6. **Incident History**:
   - Have you experienced disruptions?
   - How did you respond?
   - What was learned?

#### Stage 4: Results Analysis (Week 9)
**NQA Scoring Matrix**:

| Category | Weight | Score | Weighted Score |
|----------|--------|-------|----------------|
| BC Governance | 20% | 7/10 | 1.4 |
| BC Analysis | 15% | 8/10 | 1.2 |
| BC Strategies | 25% | 6/10 | 1.5 |
| BC Plans | 20% | 8/10 | 1.6 |
| BC Exercises | 10% | 5/10 | 0.5 |
| Incident History | 10% | 9/10 | 0.9 |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | | **7.1/10** |

**Rating Scale**:
- 9-10: Excellent (minimal risk)
- 7-8: Good (acceptable risk)
- 5-6: Adequate (requires monitoring)
- 3-4: Poor (requires improvement plan)
- 0-2: Inadequate (find alternative)

#### Stage 5: Action Planning (Week 10)
**For Adequate or Poor Ratings**:
- Request improvement plan
- Set review milestones

### 2. Internal Audit Execution Flow

**NQA Detailed Audit Process** (Per Department/Process):

#### Opening Meeting (30 minutes)
**Agenda**:
1. Welcome and introductions (5 min)
2. Audit scope and objectives (5 min)
3. Audit schedule review (5 min)
4. Logistics and housekeeping (5 min)
5. Audit methodology explanation (5 min)
6. Q&A (5 min)

**Auditor Preparation**:
- Prepare opening meeting slides
- Print agendas for all attendees
- Prepare audit checklists
- Set up recording method (notes/laptop)

#### Evidence Gathering (2-6 hours depending on scope)

**NQA Audit Questioning Technique**:

**Level 1: Understanding Questions**
- "Can you explain the process for X?"
- "Walk me through how you do Y?"
- "What happens when Z occurs?"
- "Who is responsible for this?"

**Level 2: Evidence Questions**
- "Can you show me an example?"
- "Where is this documented?"
- "What records do you keep?"
- "When was this last done?"

**Level 3: Verification Questions**
- "How do you know this is effective?"
- "What would happen if this failed?"
- "How have you improved this?"
- "What problems have you encountered?"

**Level 4: Conformance Questions**
- "How does this meet clause X requirement?"
- "Where is the evidence for Y?"
- "How is Z monitored?"
- "What are the results?"

**Audit Sampling Guidelines**:
- **Documents**: Review 3-5 examples
- **Records**: Sample last 3 months minimum
- **Personnel**: Interview 3-5 people per area
- **Observations**: Witness at least 1 example

#### Findings Analysis (30-60 minutes)
**Categorization**:

**Non-Conformity**: Failure to meet a requirement
- **Major**: Systemic failure or complete absence of requirement
- **Minor**: Isolated or occasional failure

**Observation/OFI**: Improvement opportunity but not non-conforming

**Positive Finding**: Noteworthy good practice

**NQA Finding Statement Structure**:
```
[Category]: [Clause Reference] - [Requirement]
Evidence: [What was found/not found]
Consequence: [Why it matters]
Example: [Specific instance]
```

Example:
```
Minor NC: Clause 8.4.4 - BC Exercise Programme
Evidence: The BC exercise programme shows exercises planned annually, but the last exercise for the IT department was conducted 18 months ago.
Consequence: Without regular testing, the organization cannot ensure IT recovery arrangements remain effective.
Example: IT BC Plan last tested March 2022, next test scheduled March 2024.
```

#### Closing Meeting (30-45 minutes)
**Agenda**:
1. Thank you and acknowledgements (5 min)
2. Summary of audit process (5 min)
3. Positive findings presentation (10 min)
4. Non-conformities presentation (10 min)
5. Observations presentation (5 min)
6. Next steps and timelines (5 min)
7. Q&A and clarifications (5 min)

**Presentation Tips**:
- Start with positive findings
- Present non-conformities clearly but constructively
- Provide specific examples
- Allow for clarification and discussion
- Agree on timescales for corrective actions
- End on positive note

### 3. Corrective Action Management Flow

**NQA Structured Corrective Action Process**:

#### Step 1: Non-Conformity Identification
**Sources**:
- Internal audits
- External audits
- Incident reviews
- Exercise findings
- Process observations
- Stakeholder complaints

**Documentation**:
```
NON-CONFORMITY REPORT

NCR Number: NCR-2024-001
Date Raised: 15/01/2024
Raised By: Internal Auditor
Area: IT Department
Clause Reference: 8.4.4 (BC Exercises)

Description:
The IT Department's BC plan has not been tested in the last 18 months, contrary to the annual testing requirement in the BC exercise programme.

Evidence:
- Last IT BC exercise: March 2022
- Exercise programme requires annual testing
- Current date: January 2024
- Gap: 10 months overdue

Immediate Action Required:
Schedule and conduct IT BC exercise within 30 days.

Category: Minor Non-Conformity
Priority: High
Assigned To: IT Manager
Due Date: 14/02/2024
```

#### Step 2: Root Cause Analysis
**5 Whys Technique**:
```
Problem: IT BC plan not tested

Why 1: Why wasn't the exercise conducted?
Answer: It wasn't scheduled

Why 2: Why wasn't it scheduled?
Answer: Exercise coordinator didn't track due dates

Why 3: Why didn't coordinator track it?
Answer: No tracking system in place

Why 4: Why no tracking system?
Answer: Process relies on manual calendar

Why 5: Why manual system?
Answer: No automation considered

ROOT CAUSE: Lack of systematic tracking process for BC exercises
```

**Alternative RCA Methods**:
- **Fishbone Diagram**: For complex issues with multiple causes
- **Fault Tree Analysis**: For sequential failures
- **Barrier Analysis**: For control failures

#### Step 3: Corrective Action Planning
**Action Types**:
1. **Immediate Correction**: Fix the specific instance
2. **Corrective Action**: Address root cause to prevent recurrence
3. **Preventive Action**: Identify and address similar risks

**Corrective Action Plan**:
```
CORRECTIVE ACTION PLAN

NCR Number: NCR-2024-001

Immediate Correction:
Action: Conduct IT BC desktop exercise
Owner: IT Manager
Due: 14/02/2024
Resources: BC Coordinator support, 10 IT staff, 4 hours
Cost: Internal time only

Corrective Action (Root Cause):
Action: Implement automated BC exercise tracking system
Owner: BC Manager
Due: 31/03/2024
Resources: Excel template or software tool, integration with calendar
Cost: 40 hours development + tool cost (if applicable)

Steps:
1. Research tracking tool options (1 week)
2. Select and procure tool (1 week)
3. Configure tool with all BC exercise dates (1 week)
4. Set up automatic reminders (1 week)
5. Train exercise coordinator (1 day)
6. Test system (1 week)
7. Implement across all BC exercises (1 week)

Preventive Action:
Action: Review all BC processes for adequate tracking systems
Owner: BC Manager
Due: 30/04/2024
Resources: BC team
Cost: 20 hours

Verification Method:
- Evidence of IT exercise completion
- Tracking system demonstration
- 3 months of operation showing timely exercises
- Internal audit verification

Approved By: _________________ Date: __________
```

#### Step 4: Implementation
**Week-by-Week**:
- Week 1: Immediate correction executed
- Week 2-3: Tool research and selection
- Week 4-5: Tool configuration
- Week 6-7: Testing and training
- Week 8: Full implementation

**Progress Tracking**:
- Weekly status updates
- Milestone completion
- Issue escalation if delayed
- Resource reallocation if needed

#### Step 5: Verification
**Verification Criteria**:
1. **Completion**: All planned actions completed?
2. **Effectiveness**: Root cause eliminated?
3. **Sustainment**: Process embedded and ongoing?

**Verification Methods**:
- **Observation**: Watch process in action
- **Interview**: Ask those involved
- **Records Review**: Check evidence
- **Re-audit**: Formal verification

**Verification Timeline**:
- Immediate: 1 week after correction
- Short-term: 1 month after corrective action
- Long-term: 3 months after corrective action

#### Step 6: Closure
**Closure Checklist**:
- [ ] All actions completed
- [ ] Evidence of completion documented
- [ ] Effectiveness verified
- [ ] Lessons learned captured
- [ ] Process updated if needed
- [ ] Training provided if needed
- [ ] NCR formally closed

### 2. Risk Assessment Template Workflow

**NQA Systematic Risk Assessment** (3-4 Weeks):

#### Week 1: Risk Identification
**Risk Identification Template**:

```
RISK REGISTER

Risk ID: [Auto-number]
Date Identified: [DD/MM/YYYY]
Identified By: [Name]

Risk Description: [Clear description of what could go wrong]
Category: [Select: Natural / Technical / Human / Organizational / External]
Source: [Where did we identify this? BIA / Historical / Brainstorm / External]

Affected Activities: [List from BIA]
Affected Assets: [Systems / Data / People / Facilities]

Current Controls: [What we do now to prevent/mitigate]

Impact if Occurs: [Describe consequence]
Likelihood: [Select: 1-Rare / 2-Unlikely / 3-Possible / 4-Likely / 5-Almost Certain]
Impact: [Select: 1-Insignificant / 2-Minor / 3-Moderate / 4-Major / 5-Severe]
Risk Score: [Auto-calculate: Likelihood x Impact]
Risk Level: [Auto: <5 Low / 5-9 Medium / 10-15 High / 16+ Critical]

Treatment Decision: [Accept / Reduce / Transfer / Avoid]
Treatment Actions Required: [List actions]
Treatment Owner: [Name]
Treatment Due Date: [DD/MM/YYYY]

Status: [Open / In Progress / Completed]
Last Reviewed: [DD/MM/YYYY]
Next Review: [DD/MM/YYYY]
```

**Risk Identification Methods**:

**Method 1: BIA-Driven** (Day 1-2)
- Review BIA findings
- List threats to each critical activity
- Consider dependencies at risk
- Note historical incidents

**Method 2: Brainstorming** (Day 3)
- Workshop with cross-functional team
- Use categories to prompt thinking:
  - Natural (weather, earthquake, flood, fire)
  - Technical (IT failure, power loss, equipment breakdown)
  - Human (error, absence, skills shortage, malicious)
  - Organizational (supplier failure, cash flow, change)
  - External (political, economic, legal, social)

**Method 3: Historical Review** (Day 4)
- Review past incidents in organization
- Industry incident reports
- News and media (similar organizations)
- Regulatory breach reports

**Method 4: Scenario Analysis** (Day 5)
- "What if" scenarios
- Cascading failure analysis
- Best/worst case thinking

**Target**: Identify 30-50 relevant risks minimum

#### Week 2: Risk Analysis
**For Each Risk**:

**Step 1: Determine Likelihood** (Use consistent criteria)
```
Rating | Definition | Frequency | Probability
-------|------------|-----------|------------
1 - Rare | May occur only in exceptional circumstances | < Once per 10 years | < 5%
2 - Unlikely | Could occur at some time | Once per 5-10 years | 5-25%
3 - Possible | Might occur at some time | Once per 2-5 years | 25-50%
4 - Likely | Will probably occur | Once per 1-2 years | 50-75%
5 - Almost Certain | Expected to occur | Multiple times per year | > 75%
```

**Step 2: Assess Impact** (Use consistent criteria aligned to BIA)
```
Rating | Financial | Operational | Reputation | Legal | H&S
-------|-----------|-------------|------------|-------|-----
1 - Insignificant | <£10k | Minimal | None | None | First aid
2 - Minor | £10k-£50k | Small delay | Local | Minor breach | Medical treatment
3 - Moderate | £50k-£250k | Significant disruption | Regional | Prosecution risk | Lost time injury
4 - Major | £250k-£1M | Major disruption | National | Regulatory action | Major injury
5 - Severe | >£1M | Unable to operate | International | Serious prosecution | Fatality
```

**Step 3: Calculate Risk Score**
- Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact
- Range: 1 (minimum) to 25 (maximum)

**Step 4: Plot on Risk Matrix**
```
IMPACT →
   ↑    1   2   3   4   5
   5    5  10  15  20  25
L  4    4   8  12  16  20
I  3    3   6   9  12  15
K  2    2   4   6   8  10
E  1    1   2   3   4   5
```

**Risk Levels**:
- **1-4 (Green)**: Low - Monitor, accept
- **5-9 (Yellow)**: Medium - Manage, reduce
- **10-15 (Orange)**: High - Urgent action required
- **16-25 (Red)**: Critical - Immediate action essential

#### Week 3: Risk Treatment Planning
**Treatment Options**:

**1. AVOID** (Eliminate the risk)
- Stop the activity
- Change the process
- Find alternative approach
- Example: Stop using single supplier, dual source instead

**2. REDUCE** (Implement controls)
- Preventive controls (stop it happening)
- Detective controls (find it quickly)
- Corrective controls (fix it fast)
- Example: Install UPS to prevent power disruption

**3. TRANSFER** (Share the risk)
- Insurance
- Outsourcing
- Contractual terms
- Example: Cyber insurance policy

**4. ACCEPT** (Live with the risk)
- Risk is low
- Cost of treatment exceeds benefit
- No viable options
- Example: Low probability, low impact risks

**Treatment Plan Template**:
```
RISK TREATMENT PLAN

Risk ID: [R-001]
Risk Description: [Fire in main building]
Current Risk Level: Critical (20)

Treatment Strategy: REDUCE

Preventive Controls:
[ ] Fire detection system installed
[ ] Regular fire safety inspections
[ ] Fire safety training for all staff
[ ] Hot work permits enforced
[ ] Smoking ban policy
[ ] Electrical safety testing

Detective Controls:
[ ] 24/7 monitoring of fire alarms
[ ] Regular alarm tests
[ ] Security cameras (fire detection)

Corrective Controls:
[ ] Fire suppression system
[ ] Emergency evacuation procedures
[ ] Fire wardens appointed and trained
[ ] Assembly points defined
[ ] Alternative site identified
[ ] BC plan includes fire scenario

Target Risk Level: Medium (8)

Implementation:
Action | Owner | Cost | Due Date | Status
-------|-------|------|----------|-------
Install sprinklers | Facilities | £50k | 31/03/24 | In progress
Fire warden training | H&S | £2k | 15/02/24 | Complete
BC plan fire section | BC Mgr | Internal | 28/02/24 | Complete

Total Investment: £52k + internal time
Expected Benefit: Reduce likelihood from 5 to 2
Residual Risk: 2 (likelihood) x 4 (impact) = 8 (Medium)

Review Date: 31/12/2024
```
